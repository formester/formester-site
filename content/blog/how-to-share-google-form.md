**Google Forms** is a great tool for creating **[surveys](/templates/categories/survey-forms/)**, quizzes, registration forms, and collecting feedback. It’s simple to use, free, and works for almost any type of form. But sometimes, sharing your form so people actually respond can feel tricky.

In this guide, we’ll cover the easiest ways to share your Google Forms so you can get responses faster and with less hassle.

### 1. Share Your Form via Link

The simplest way to share a Google Form is by using a link.

- Open your form and click the **Send** button in the top right corner.  
- Copy the link provided. You can also shorten the link to make it easier to share.  
- Paste the link anywhere; emails, blogs, social media posts, or chat apps.  

This method is perfect for a quick and simple way to reach anyone online. People can open the link and start submitting responses immediately without extra steps.

### 2. Embed the Form on Your Website

If you want people to fill out your form directly on your website, **[embedding](/blog/how-to-embed-a-google-form/)** it is the best option.

- Click the **Send** button, then choose the **embed code** option.  
- Copy the HTML code provided.  
- Paste the code into your website editor or page builder.  

Embedding your form keeps people on your page and gives a more professional look. It works great for blogs, landing pages, or business sites where you want to keep visitors engaged.

### 3. Send the Form Directly via Email

You can also send your Google Form straight to people’s inboxes:

- Click the **email icon** in the Send menu.  
- Enter the email addresses of your recipients.  
- Add a custom message if you like and hit **Send**.  

This method is great if you have a list of respondents. It helps you keep everything organized in Gmail or Google Workspace.

### Bonus Tip: Control Responses and Track Submissions

Google Forms lets you manage who can respond and track responses:

- Limit responses to specific people or email addresses.  
- Allow only one response per person.  
- Set deadlines for submissions.  

These settings help you collect data more efficiently and avoid messy or duplicate responses.

<div class="fmstr-cmp-bef-faq__container">
<h2 class="fmstr-cmp-bef-faq__h2" id="fmstr-cmp-bef-faq-h2">Frequently asked questions</h2>
<p class="fmstr-cmp-bef-faq__intro">Common questions about enterprise procurement, SOC 2, SSO, HIPAA, and self-hosting.</p>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bef-faq__list">

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bef-faq__item"><summary>What makes a form builder enterprise-grade?</summary><div class="fmstr-cmp-bef-faq__answer">Minimum bar: SOC 2 Type II, SAML SSO, audit logs, dedicated CSM, custom DPA, actual SLA. Leading enterprise builders also offer RBAC, custom branding, white-label, and HIPAA or self-hosted deployment for stricter use cases.</div></details>
<details class="fmstr-cmp-bef-faq__item"><summary>Do enterprise form builders need SSO?</summary><div class="fmstr-cmp-bef-faq__answer">Yes. SAML SSO with Okta / Azure AD / Google Workspace is table stakes; OIDC is becoming standard. All 10 builders support SSO on enterprise tiers.</div></details>
<details class="fmstr-cmp-bef-faq__item"><summary>SOC 2 vs HIPAA, which do I need?</summary><div class="fmstr-cmp-bef-faq__answer">SOC 2 Type II is the broad enterprise standard (security controls). HIPAA is healthcare-specific (PHI). SOC 2 is sufficient for most enterprises; HIPAA only required for PHI. Formstack, Jotform Enterprise, FormAssembly, Cognito, 123FormBuilder are HIPAA-eligible; Formester is not.</div></details>
<details class="fmstr-cmp-bef-faq__item"><summary>Worth it for mid-market teams?</summary><div class="fmstr-cmp-bef-faq__answer">Yes if you have 50+ employees, need SSO, have a procurement team that asks for SOC 2, or have data sensitivity. Under 50 employees, mid-tier ($30-99/mo) plans usually suffice.</div></details>
<details class="fmstr-cmp-bef-faq__item"><summary>Pricing range?</summary><div class="fmstr-cmp-bef-faq__answer">Public reports place enterprise tiers at $99-$300/user/year for Jotform Enterprise and similar. Formstack Enterprise is custom-quoted, typically $5,000-$50,000/year. Formester Enterprise is volume-based, not per-seat.</div></details>
<details class="fmstr-cmp-bef-faq__item"><summary>Self-hosted options?</summary><div class="fmstr-cmp-bef-faq__answer">Form.io and Tripetto are the only mainstream options with self-hosting (Docker / Kubernetes for Form.io; JS library for Tripetto). The SaaS-only vendors don't offer self-host.</div></details>
<details class="fmstr-cmp-bef-faq__item"><summary>Salesforce integration?</summary><div class="fmstr-cmp-bef-faq__answer">FormAssembly has native Salesforce object mapping. Formstack, Jotform Enterprise, and Fillout integrate via native connectors. The rest use Zapier or webhooks.</div></details>
<details class="fmstr-cmp-bef-faq__item"><summary>Audit logs and compliance reporting?</summary><div class="fmstr-cmp-bef-faq__answer">Formstack, Jotform Enterprise, FormAssembly, Form.io, and Microsoft Forms (via M365) all have detailed audit logs accessible to admins. Verify specific log fields your compliance team needs before signing.</div></details>
<details class="fmstr-cmp-bef-faq__item"><summary>Enterprise procurement timeline?</summary><div class="fmstr-cmp-bef-faq__answer">Expect 30-90 days for vendor onboarding (security review, DPA, SSO, SLA). Mature enterprise sales (Formstack, Jotform Enterprise, FormAssembly) move faster with standard contracts.</div></details>
<details class="fmstr-cmp-bef-faq__item"><summary>Free enterprise option?</summary><div class="fmstr-cmp-bef-faq__answer">Form.io's open-source community edition is genuinely free for self-hosted use. Microsoft Forms is 'free' if you already pay for M365 enterprise. No true SaaS enterprise option is free.</div></details>

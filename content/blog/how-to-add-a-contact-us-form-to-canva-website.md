Want to turn your Canva website into a lead-generating machine? 

All you need is a contact form. In this guide, I’ll walk you through <strong>how to add a Contact Us form to your Canva website</strong> using Formester, a <strong><a href="/plugins/canva-form-builder/">free no-code form builder for Canva</a></strong> that makes everything easy, even if you're not techy.

## Why Use Canva for Your Website?
If you're just starting out and don’t want the hassle of coding, Canva is a great choice for building your website. It’s fast, free, and beginner-friendly. 

Here’s why so many people choose it:

- <strong>No Code Needed</strong>

You don’t need to know any coding. Just drag and drop elements to build your site the way you want. Whether it’s text, images, buttons, or icons, Canva makes it simple.

- <strong>Free Templates</strong>

Canva gives you access to hundreds of professionally designed website templates. You can explore different categories like business, portfolio, or event pages. Just pick a template that suits your brand and customize it easily.

- <strong>Easy to Design and Publish</strong>

Designing a website in Canva feels just like creating a regular Canva project. You can add new sections, change colors, upload images, and move things around without any learning curve. 

Once you’re happy with how it looks, just hit Publish. Your site goes live instantly with a shareable Canva link.

Whether you are building a landing page, a personal site, or a simple online portfolio, Canva helps you get it done quickly and with confidence.

Now let’s add that contact form!

### Step 1: Create a Landing Page in Canva
- Go to Canva.

- Search for “<strong><a href="https://www.canva.com/website-builder/">Canva Website Builder.</a></strong>”

- Choose a template or start with a blank design.

- Build your landing page by adding text, images, and branding.

### Step 2: Create a Contact Us Form in Formester
- <strong><a href="https://app.formester.com/users/sign_in">Log in to Formester</a></strong>.

- Click <strong>New Form</strong>.

- Use the AI Form Generator or build from scratch.

<strong>Add these fields:</strong>

- Name

- Phone

- Email

- Message box

### Step 3: Match the Form Design With Your Canva Page

- In <strong>Canva</strong>, copy the color code of the document colors (found in the color panel).

<strong>In Formester, customize:</strong>

- Button color

- Question text color

- Background color

- Font style and size

This keeps your form on-brand and seamless with your design.

### Step 4: Embed the Form in Canva
- In Canva, go to <strong>Apps.</strong>

- Search for and select Formester.

- Copy your form URL from Formester.

- <strong>Paste the URL</strong> in Canva’s app window.

- Adjust the form height to match the form width (for better layout).

- Resize and reposition the form as needed.

### Step 5: Publish Your Website
- Hit the <strong>Publish</strong> button.

- Open your live site.

- Do a test submission to make sure everything works smoothly.

### Features You Might Want to Use
Formester is more than just a basic form builder. It comes with <strong><a href="/features/">powerful features</a></strong> that save time, keep you organized, and help you follow up with leads. 

Here’s how these features can make your life easier:

<strong><a href="/features/email-notification-for-form-submission/">Email Notifications</a></strong>
As soon as someone submits your form, you get an instant email. No need to keep checking your dashboard. For example, if you're a freelancer and a client sends an inquiry, you’ll know right away and can respond quickly. 

This helps you act fast and never miss an opportunity.

<strong><a href="/features/autoresponder-email/">Autoresponder</a></strong>
Right after someone fills out your form, they receive an automatic email from you. This can be a thank you message, a confirmation, or even next steps. 

If you run a small business, this makes your brand look more professional and saves you from replying to every message manually.

<strong>Submissions Tab</strong>
All your form responses are stored in one place. You can view, sort, or filter them anytime. 

If you're getting a lot of entries, like job applications or event signups, this feature helps you stay organized and find specific responses without the mess of a crowded inbox or scattered spreadsheets.

<strong><a href="/integrations/">CRM Integration</a></strong>
You can connect Formester with your existing CRM tool. This means any new contact from your form goes directly into your lead database. 

If you're a marketer or agency, this saves hours of manual data entry and helps you follow up with leads faster, keeping your sales process smooth and efficient.

These features are simple to use and designed to solve real problems. Whether you're running a business, managing a team, or building a personal brand, Formester helps you stay on top of every response with less effort.

With Canva and Formester, you do not need to be a designer or developer to build a great-looking website and start collecting leads.

You can even connect your form to a CRM tool to follow up with new leads and grow your business. Design your website, connect your form, publish your page, and you’re ready to go. 

It’s fast, simple, and made for people who want results without the hassle.

<details class="fmstr-cmp-ccf-faq__item">
<summary>Can you add a contact form to a Canva website?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-ccf-faq__answer">Yes. Canva supports embedded forms through the Embed element. Build the form in any form builder, copy the embed code, paste it into Canva's Embed element, position it on the page, and publish.</div>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-ccf-faq__item">
<summary>Does Canva have a built-in contact form?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-ccf-faq__answer">Canva has a basic form element under Pro plans, but the styling and field-type options are limited. For real contact forms with required validation, email notifications, and integrations, embed an external form.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-ccf-faq__item">
<summary>How do I embed a Formester contact form in Canva?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-ccf-faq__answer">Build the contact form in Formester, click Share, copy the embed code, in Canva click Elements then Embed, paste the URL or embed code, position the form on the page, and click Publish website.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-ccf-faq__item">
<summary>Do I need Canva Pro to add a contact form?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-ccf-faq__answer">No. The Embed element is available on Canva's free plan. You only need Canva Pro for the native form element (which has limited functionality).</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-ccf-faq__item">
<summary>Why isn't my Canva embedded form showing?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-ccf-faq__answer">Most often: the embed URL is wrong, the form's site permissions block iframes, or Canva's Embed element didn't resize. Double-check the URL, allow iframe embedding in the form's settings, and resize the Canva element.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-ccf-faq__item">
<summary>Can I customize the form design to match my Canva site?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-ccf-faq__answer">Yes. In Formester, customize the form's colors, fonts, and button styles to match your Canva brand kit. The embedded form inherits your styling.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-ccf-faq__item">
<summary>Can the contact form send me an email when someone submits?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-ccf-faq__answer">Yes. Turn on email notifications in your form builder. Every submission triggers an email to your inbox with the respondent's name, email, and message.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-ccf-faq__item">
<summary>Can I add a CAPTCHA to a Canva contact form?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-ccf-faq__answer">Yes if you use an external form builder. Formester includes built-in spam protection (reCAPTCHA-style) on all plans. Canva's native form does not.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-ccf-faq__item">
<summary>How do I track form conversions on a Canva website?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-ccf-faq__answer">Add Google Analytics or a Meta Pixel via Canva's Code element, then route form-submission events to that pixel. Or use the form builder's built-in analytics.</div>
</details>

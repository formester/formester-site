<h2 class="fmstr-cmp-bof-body__h2">Formester: Best all-rounder (web + offline + payments in one)</h2>
<p class="fmstr-cmp-bof-body__tagline">Best for: Teams that want one form builder for lead capture, payments, surveys, and offline data collection without per-user pricing.</p>

<div class="fmstr-cmp-bof-body__row">
<div>
<p class="fmstr-cmp-bof-body__block-title">How offline works</p>
<p class="fmstr-cmp-bof-body__p">Formester forms cache in the browser. The respondent fills the form with no internet; every answer is saved locally and queued. Everything uploads automatically once the device reconnects. <a href="/features/offline-forms/">See how offline forms work →</a></p>
</div>
<div>
<p class="fmstr-cmp-bof-body__block-title">Key features</p>
<ul class="fmstr-cmp-bof-body__list">
<li>Offline form filling in the browser (no separate app)</li>
<li>Auto-sync on reconnect</li>
<li>AI form generator, conditional logic, payments</li>
<li>GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA compliant</li>
<li>Real-time team collaboration on Business plan</li>
</ul>
</div>
</div>

<p class="fmstr-cmp-bof-body__block-title">Pricing</p>
<ul class="fmstr-cmp-bof-body__pricing">
<li><strong>Free Plan:</strong> Yes (100 responses/month, unlimited forms)</li>
<li><strong>Starting Price:</strong> $13/month Personal ($12/month annual)</li>
<li><strong>Most Popular Plan:</strong> $49/month Business ($45/month annual)</li>
<li><strong>Enterprise Plan:</strong> Custom pricing</li>
<li><strong>Free Trial:</strong> No (free plan available instead)</li>
<li><strong>Response Limits:</strong> 100 (free) / 5,000 (Personal) / 15,000 (Business)</li>
<li><strong>Storage:</strong> 100MB free / 1GB Personal / 50GB Business</li>
<li><strong>Team Collaboration:</strong> Business plan and above</li>
</ul>

<div class="fmstr-cmp-bof-body__proscons">
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bof-body__pros">
<strong>Pros</strong>
Web-first, no per-device limits. Single tool for online + offline. AI + offline combo is unique. Honest free plan.
</div>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bof-body__cons">
<strong>Cons</strong>
Not a native mobile app (browser-cached). Not HIPAA-certified, use Jotform Sign or Fulcrum if you handle PHI.

<section class="fmstr-cmp-bof-faq" aria-labelledby="fmstr-cmp-bof-faq-h2">
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bof-faq__container">
<h2 class="fmstr-cmp-bof-faq__h2" id="fmstr-cmp-bof-faq-h2">Frequently asked questions</h2>
<p class="fmstr-cmp-bof-faq__intro">Common questions about offline form builders, sync behavior, and use cases.</p>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bof-faq__list">

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bof-faq__item">
<summary>What is an offline form builder?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bof-faq__answer">An offline form builder is a form-creation tool whose forms can be filled out and submitted on a device with no internet connection. Submissions are saved locally on the device and queued; when the device reconnects, everything syncs to the cloud automatically. The respondent doesn't have to do anything special.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bof-faq__item">
<summary>Do offline forms really save data if the device dies before sync?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bof-faq__answer">Yes, in most tools listed above. Formester, Jotform Mobile Forms, Fulcrum, GoCanvas, Zoho Forms, KoboToolbox, ProntoForms, and Device Magic store submissions in the device's local storage (SQLite or equivalent), so even if the battery dies, the data survives. Microsoft Forms is the exception: its offline mode is browser cache only, and data is lost if the browser is closed before reconnecting.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bof-faq__item">
<summary>Is there a free offline form builder?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bof-faq__answer">Yes. Formester is free for up to 100 responses/month. Jotform Mobile Forms is free for 100 submissions/month. KoboToolbox is free for qualifying nonprofits and self-hosted use. Zoho Forms is free for 3 forms / 500 submissions/month.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bof-faq__item">
<summary>What's the difference between an offline form and a mobile form?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bof-faq__answer">A mobile form is any form designed to work on phones and tablets. An offline form is a form that works without an internet connection. All offline forms are mobile-friendly, but not all mobile forms work offline. If you specifically need no-signal data collection, look for "offline mode" in the product page (not just "mobile-friendly").</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bof-faq__item">
<summary>Can I use offline forms for trade shows and events?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bof-faq__answer">Yes. Trade shows are the textbook use case. The exhibit hall Wi-Fi is usually saturated, so booth staff can collect leads offline on a tablet or phone, and submissions sync to your CRM when the device hits LTE or hotel Wi-Fi later. Jotform's kiosk mode and Formester's cached browser forms both handle this well.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bof-faq__item">
<summary>Do offline forms work on iPad and Android tablets?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bof-faq__answer">Yes, with caveats. Native apps (Jotform Mobile Forms, Fulcrum, GoCanvas, KoboCollect) work on both iPad and Android tablets. Web-first tools (Formester, Zoho Forms via browser, Microsoft Forms) work on both via the browser; cache the form first, then disconnect.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bof-faq__item">
<summary>How do offline form builders handle file uploads?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bof-faq__answer">The leading tools queue photos and signatures locally, then upload them with the form submission once online. File-size limits matter: large photos can hit upload caps. Check the per-plan file-size limit before committing. Formester's Business plan allows 50GB total storage and 1GB per file.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bof-faq__item">
<summary>Are offline form builders GDPR-compliant?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bof-faq__answer">The leading tools (Formester, Jotform, Zoho Forms, Fulcrum, GoCanvas) are GDPR-compliant out of the box. Some also offer DPAs for enterprise. For HIPAA, only Jotform Sign, Fulcrum (with the right plan), and ProntoForms have BAAs available; Formester is GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA only and does not sign BAAs.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bof-faq__item">
<summary>What about data security on offline-stored submissions?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bof-faq__answer">Tools that take security seriously encrypt local-device storage. KoboToolbox uses ODK's standard encryption. Fulcrum and ProntoForms support device-level encryption (AES-256 in transit and at rest). For the highest stakes, check whether the tool can wipe queued submissions remotely if the device is lost.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bof-faq__item">
<summary>Can offline forms handle conditional logic and required fields?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bof-faq__answer">Yes, all the leading tools run conditional logic locally on the device. Required-field validation works the same offline as online. The form refuses to submit until required fields are filled, just as it would on the web.</div>
</details>

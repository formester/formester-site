Canva is a fantastic tool for designing visuals, but it falls short when it comes to building interactive, fillable forms. 

While **[Canva form builder](https://www.canva.com/create/forms/)** allows users to create beautiful PDFs and static form templates, it lacks key features like data collection, automation, conditional logic, and payment integrations.

If you're looking for a **[Canva alternative for form building](/plugins/canva-form-builder/)**, you need a tool that not only looks great but also functions effectively.

In this post, we’ll explore five of the **best Canva form builder alternatives** that offer easy-to-use, visually appealing, and highly functional form-building capabilities.

## What Makes a Great Canva Alternative for Forms?
Before diving into the alternatives, let’s break down the essential features a good form builder should have:

- **Easy Drag-and-Drop Design** – Like Canva, a great form builder should allow you to create beautiful forms without any coding.

- **Data Collection & Automation** – Forms should store responses, integrate with Google Sheets, and automate tasks like sending emails.

- **Custom Branding** – You should be able to personalize colors, logos, and themes to match your brand.

- **Interactive Features** – Unlike Canva, a form tool should support conditional logic, file uploads, e-signatures, and payments.

- **Share & Embed Options** – Forms should work well on websites, social media, and mobile devices.

Now, let’s check out the best tools that offer all these features and more!

### 2. Jotform – Best for Advanced Customization
![a mockup of jotform](https://formester-strapi.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/jotform_0ac615425e.png)

Jotform is another strong alternative to Canva for creating interactive, data-driven forms. It provides thousands of templates, advanced integrations, and automation features.

**Key Features:**

- Massive library of form templates

- HIPAA-compliant forms for healthcare and legal industries

- Integration with over 1000+ tools like Google Drive, PayPal, and CRMs

- Payment collection and approval workflows

**Who is it for?**
 Businesses that need highly customizable forms with automation and advanced security.

**Pricing:**
 **Free plan** available, but paid plans start at **$34 per month**.

### 3. Typeform – Best for Conversational Forms
![a mockup of typeform](https://formester-strapi.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/typeform_911b500df0.png)

Typeform is perfect if you want forms that feel like real conversations rather than just static questionnaires. Unlike Canva, Typeform presents one question at a time, making forms more engaging.

**Key Features:**

- One-question-at-a-time layout for better user experience

- Custom branding and design flexibility

- Works well for surveys, quizzes, and feedback collection

- Integrates with Slack, Google Sheets, and Mailchimp

**Who is it for?**
 Businesses and marketers who want highly engaging, conversational forms for surveys, quizzes, and lead generation.

**Pricing:**
 Free plan available; paid plans start at **$25 per month**.

### 4. Paperform – Best for Payment & Order Forms
![a mockup of paperform](https://formester-strapi.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/paperform_408f91f490.png)

Paperform is a great alternative if you're looking for a tool that combines form-building with rich text editing and media embedding. It's like a mix of Canva and a form builder!

**Key Features:**

- Visually rich, customizable form layouts

- Accepts payments directly through Stripe and PayPal

- Embeds videos, images, and custom branding elements

- Smart form logic and automation options

**Who is it for?**
 Small businesses, freelancers, and e-commerce sellers who need forms with payment collection.

**Pricing:**
 Paid plans start at **$24 per month**.

### 5. Google Forms – Best Free Alternative

![a mockup of google forms](https://formester-strapi.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/google_forms_5719259c33.png)

If you’re on a budget and need a simple, no-frills alternative to Canva, Google Forms is a solid choice. While it doesn’t offer advanced branding or AI-powered features, it’s completely free and integrates well with Google Sheets.

**Key Features:**

- 100% free with unlimited responses

- Integrates with Google Sheets for easy data collection

- Supports quizzes, surveys, and feedback forms

- No design customization, but great for quick form creation

**Who is it for?**
 Educators, students, and small teams who need a free, easy-to-use form builder.

**Pricing:**
 Completely free for all users.

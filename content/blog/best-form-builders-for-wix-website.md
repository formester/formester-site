## What is a Form Builder?
A form builder is a tool that helps you create online forms like contact forms, registration forms, and application forms without any coding. Whether you need a survey, quiz, or order form, it simplifies the process.

You can use drag-and-drop features to add form fields like text boxes, drop-down menus, or signature fields, making it easy to collect specific information.

Once your form is ready, embed it on your website or share it via a link to start gathering responses. 
>Learn more about **[what form builders are](/blog/what-is-a-form-builder/)** in detail.

### How to Choose a Form Builder
Choosing the right form builder is key. A good tool saves time, helps you collect data easily, and lets you customize your form to match your brand. Here’s what to consider:

#### 1. Ease of Use
Choose a tool that’s easy to use. Look for simple drag-and-drop features, ready-made templates, and clear designs. 
Don’t know coding? No worries! The best tools are beginner-friendly and come with guides or tutorials to help you.

#### 2. Customization
Your forms should match your brand. Choose a builder that lets you change colors, fonts, and add logos. Mobile-friendly designs and white-label options (no builder branding) are a bonus.

#### 3. Integrations
Check if the form connects with your favorite tools, like email marketing software or payment apps. Syncing with tools like Mailchimp or PayPal can save you tons of time.

#### 4. Features
Decide what you need: payment collection, smart forms (like showing extra questions only when needed), or AI-powered forms that build themselves. Look for features that match your goals.

#### 5. Other Considerations
Think about cost, security (data protection), and customer support. Make sure it fits your budget, keeps your data safe, and has helpful support in case you’re stuck.

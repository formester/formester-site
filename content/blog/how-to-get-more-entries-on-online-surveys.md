Online surveys – they are thought of as one of the most powerful tools that market researchers use to gather data. But it is not just about creating a well-designed survey. 

The real challenge is to get the audience to fill out the form. Low participation can undermine the quality of your insights. If that happens, you’ll be left with biased or incomplete data. 

If you are a market researcher and have ever struggled to get enough responses to your survey forms, this blog post is for you. In this blog post, you’ll learn some pro-level tested tips that can help you increase the visibility of your online survey forms. 

## 1. Optimize Survey Design to Increase Engagement
Before sharing your survey with the public, take a moment to optimize it. For this, observe:

- Is it visually appealing?

- Is it concise or not?

- Is it easy to complete?

These are the things that can significantly impact audience participation rates. Know that people are less likely to complete forms that are long, chunky, or complicated. 

If you're unsure how to start optimizing, check out these seven assured ways to **[improve your online surveys](/blog/seven-assured-ways-to-improve-your-online-surveys/)** to boost engagement and responses.

### Keep It Simple and Focused
To get maximum participation, it is suggested to keep the design of the form simple and user focused. Nobody today has the time to scroll through endless pages of questions. 

SurveyMonkey suggested keeping the length of the online survey **[under 10 minutes](https://www.surveymonkey.com/curiosity/survey_completion_times/)** if high engagement is what you desire. This can only be done if you stick to clear and specific questions. Only those that target the key data you want to collect. 

### Mobile-Friendly Format
Statistics shared by DataReportal in February 2025 mention that there are around 7.4 billion smartphones in use worldwide. This number is huge. But why are we telling you this? 

Because a considerable portion of participants will access your surveys through smartphones. Ensure your survey form is optimized for mobile devices.

## 2. Use Social Media for Survey Promotion
Social media platforms are a treasure trove for increasing survey participation. However, it only works when you approach the audience strategically. 

Many researchers drop a survey link into a random post and hope for the best. This will get them no results. But a targeted and engaging plan will yield far better results.

Below is how you can attract more audience to participate in your survey on social media. 

### Generate Attention-Grabbing Captions
Social media moves fast, so crafting engaging captions for your posts is crucial. To craft engaging captions, get assistance from **[Paragraphgenerator.io](https://www.paragraphgenerator.io/)**. This tool will provide you with quality content upon giving a prompt. 

Once you have gotten the content from the tool, give it a compelling structure that can boost your post's visibility. Here’s how you can do that:


- State the benefits

- Add a call to action

- Make use of emojis

- Make use of line breaks

### Choose the Right Platform
Identify which platforms your target audience is most active on. Is your audience on LinkedIn for professional surveys or on Instagram for consumer-oriented ones? Tailor your posts to fit the culture of the platform.

Besides this, you can also promote your survey using paid social ad campaigns. Platforms, i.e., Facebook and Instagram allow focused targeting to ensure your survey reaches the right demographics for better responses.

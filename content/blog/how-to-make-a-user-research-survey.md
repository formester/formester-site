A **user research survey** helps you collect important information about your audience. This way, you can improve your **product or service** using real user feedback.

If you want to measure **[customer satisfaction](/templates/customer-satisfaction-survey-696/)**, test a new feature, or improve your design, a good survey helps. It makes sure you gather useful data.

In this guide, you will learn **how to create a user research survey**. We will cover effective **research methods**, the **best survey question types](/blog/best-survey-questions/)**, and **[survey practices](https://www.surveymonkey.com/learn/survey-best-practices/)**. These tips will help you get useful results.

## Steps to create a User Research Survey
Follow the simple steps below to create highly engaging user research surveys that will motivate users to provide real feedback.

### Step 1: Define Your Research Goal
Start by deciding what you want to learn. Are you looking for **qualitative insights** about user behavior, or do you need **quantitative data** to measure trends? 

Defining your goal will help you choose the **right research tool** and the **[best types of surveys](https://inmoment.com/blog/types-of-surveys/)** to use.

### Step 2: Identify Your Target Audience
To get relevant feedback, you need to **recruit participants** who fit your ideal user profile. This could be new customers, experienced users, or people facing specific **pain points** with your **product or service**.

### Step 3: Choose the Right Question Types
The **type of question** you ask affects the quality of responses.

- **Open-ended questions** allow users to share detailed feedback in their own words. These are great for uncovering unexpected insights and improving **user experiences**.

- **Closed-ended questions** provide predefined answer choices, making it easier to collect quantitative data and analyze trends.

- A mix of both helps balance **qualitative data** with measurable statistics.

Good question types include rating scales, multiple-choice, and ranking questions. If you need deeper insights, consider supplementing your survey with user interviews for a more detailed perspective.

### Step 4: Select a Research Tool
Using the **right research tools** makes survey creation and **data collection** easier. Formester is a great research tool for building surveys quickly. It offers a **[free AI survey generator](/ai-survey-generator/)** which you can use to generate any kind of survey with a simple prompt.

Google Forms is free but lacks advanced features. SurveyMonkey offers interactive designs but can be expensive for premium users.

### Step 5: Distribute the Survey
Once your survey is ready, share it through multiple channels. Email, social media, and **[qr codes](/blog/how-to-create-and-share-qr-codes-for-your-forms/)** can also work well. If you're running a research study, in-app surveys and direct outreach can help you reach engaged users.

### Step 6: Collect and Analyze Survey Data
After data collection, analyze your survey data to find trends and patterns. Identify common pain points users mention. If multiple respondents struggle with the same issue, it’s a clear signal for improvement.

Examine both the detailed answers from open-ended questions and the numbers from rating scales. This will give you a full view.

### Best Practices for Better User Research Surveys
Follow these best practices to improve response rates:

- Keep the survey short and focused.
- Use clear, simple language.
- Offer incentives if possible.
- Avoid leading or biased questions.

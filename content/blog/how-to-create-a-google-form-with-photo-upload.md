Sometimes, you may need people to upload a photo when filling out a form. For example, if you’re **collecting ID proofs**, profile pictures, or event entries, a photo upload option makes the process easy.

## Here’s how you can let users upload photos using Google Forms.

### Step 1: Open or Create Your Google Form

- Go to **[Google Forms](https://forms.google.com)**

- Start a new form or open one you already made.

### Step 2: Add a File Upload Question

1. Click the **plus button** on the side to add a new question.

2. From the dropdown, choose "**File upload**" as the question type.

3. Google will tell you it’s going to collect uploaded files in your Google Drive. Click **Continue**.

Now your question will allow users to **upload files**.

### Step 3: Limit to Photos Only

1. Under "**File types allowed**", check only **Images**.

2. You can also set the **maximum number of files** and the **maximum file size**.

3. Add a clear question title like: "**Please upload your photo**"

That’s it, users can now upload photos when they fill out your form.

### Things to Know

- Users must be signed in with a Google account to upload files

- Uploaded photos will go to a folder in your Google Drive

- You can't use file upload on public forms without login

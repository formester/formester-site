<p style="font-size: inherit;">ChatGPT cannot open Google Forms, so "create a Google Form with ChatGPT" means one of three things: Gemini's Help me create a form button inside Google Forms, ChatGPT writing a Google Apps Script that builds the form for you, or a ChatGPT add-on from the Google Workspace Marketplace.</p>

<p>The first is fastest if your account has it. The second works on any Google account and takes about five minutes.</p>

<p>This page walks through all three, with the prompt that gets usable questions and the point where each route stops. The survey version, with its own ten-step flow, lives on <a href="/blog/how-to-make-a-survey-in-google-forms-using-chat-gpt/">how to make a survey in Google Forms using ChatGPT</a>.</p>

<div class="tldr">
<p><strong>Quick answer</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Route 1, Gemini in Forms:</strong> type what you want in the Help me create a form box and click Create form. Needs an eligible Google Workspace or Google AI plan, desktop only.</li>
<li><strong>Route 2, ChatGPT plus Apps Script:</strong> ask ChatGPT for the questions, then for a Google Apps Script that creates them. Paste it at script.google.com, run it once and the form appears in Drive. Works on any Google account.</li>
<li><strong>Route 3, a Marketplace add-on:</strong> a third-party sidebar inside Google Forms that generates questions from a prompt. No code, but you grant it access to your forms.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 id="direct">Can ChatGPT create a Google Form directly?</h2>

<p>No. ChatGPT has no connection to Google Forms and cannot log into your Google account, click Blank form or add a question. What it can do is write everything a form is made of: the title, the questions, the answer options, which question type each one needs and whether it is required.</p>

<p>It can also write the code that turns that list into a real form. Google Forms has a scripting layer called Apps Script, and ChatGPT is good at producing a script that builds a form question by question. You run the script once and Google does the clicking.</p>

<h2 id="gemini">Route 1: Gemini's Help me create a form inside Google Forms</h2>

<p><img src="https://formester-strapi.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/45fb903f539ec314_gforms-gpt-route1-gemini-help-me-create.gif" alt="The Gemini Help me create a form dialog in Google Forms: typing a bakery feedback prompt, Gemini generating a preview, then Create form inserting the questions" loading="lazy"></p>

<p>If your Google account is on an eligible plan, a new form opens with a Gemini box that says Let's start creating. Type what the form is for, who fills it in and roughly how many questions you want. Gemini drafts the whole form, shows a preview, and <strong>Create form</strong> inserts it into the editor as normal questions you can edit.</p>

<p>You can also click <strong>Help me create a form</strong> in the toolbar of an empty form and point it at a file in Drive, such as a Doc or a PDF, so the questions come from that content. <strong>Recreate</strong> gives you another version if the first one misses.</p>

<p>Three things to know before you rely on it. It is only on Google Workspace Business Standard and Plus, Enterprise Standard and Plus, Education Plus and the Google AI Pro and Ultra plans, and it is rolling out gradually.</p>

<p>It works on desktop only. And it builds one page: no sections, and running it on a form that already has questions replaces them rather than adding to them.</p>

<p>Since July 2026 it can also generate a quiz, with the correct answers and points set. Google's help article on <a href="https://support.google.com/docs/answer/16346789" target="_blank" rel="noopener">creating a form with Gemini</a> lists the current plans.</p>

<h2 id="chatgpt">Route 2: ChatGPT writes the questions, Apps Script builds the form</h2>

<p>This is the route that works on a free Google account. ChatGPT does the thinking and the code, Google Apps Script does the building. Six steps, about five minutes.</p>

<ol class="steps">
<li>
<h3>Ask ChatGPT for the questions</h3>
<p><img src="https://formester-strapi.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/cbab756acfdfbe9c_gforms-gpt-step1-prompt-questions.gif" alt="Typing a prompt into ChatGPT for an eight-question bakery feedback form and ChatGPT returning the questions with a question type and options for each" loading="lazy"></p>
<p>Open <a href="https://chatgpt.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener">chatgpt.com</a> and describe the form: what it is for, who fills it in, how many questions and the answer types you want. Ask for the type next to each question. Read the list and cut or reword anything before the next step, because fixing questions here is faster than fixing them in Forms.</p>
</li>
<li>
<h3>Ask for a Google Apps Script that creates the form</h3>
<p><img src="https://formester-strapi.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/81e77fc2690d8372_gforms-gpt-step2-apps-script.gif" alt="Asking ChatGPT to turn the questions into a Google Apps Script that uses FormApp, the script appearing in a code block, then clicking Copy code" loading="lazy"></p>
<p>In the same chat, type: "Turn these questions into a Google Apps Script that creates this Google Form with FormApp. Use the right item type for each question, set the required ones to required, and log the form's edit URL." Click <strong>Copy code</strong> on the block it returns.</p>
</li>
<li>
<h3>Paste the script at script.google.com</h3>
<p>Go to <a href="https://script.google.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener">script.google.com</a>, click <strong>New project</strong>, select everything in the editor and paste over it. Give the project a name so you can find it again. Nothing has been built yet.</p>
</li>
<li>
<h3>Run it once and authorise it</h3>
<p>Make sure the function name in the dropdown matches the one in the script, then click <strong>Run</strong>. The first run asks you to review permissions, because the script is about to create a file in your Drive. Pick your account and allow it. Under the editor, the Execution log prints the edit URL when it finishes.</p>
</li>
<li>
<h3>Open the form</h3>
<p>Click the URL in the log, or open Drive and look for a new form with the title from the script. Every question ChatGPT listed is now a real Google Forms question with its type and options set.</p>
</li>
<li>
<h3>Finish in Google Forms</h3>
<p>The script builds questions, not a finished form. Add a description, pick a theme and go through Settings for the parts scripts rarely cover: collecting email addresses, limiting to one response and the confirmation message. Then <strong>Publish</strong> and copy the responder link. <a href="/blog/how-to-use-google-forms/">How to use Google Forms</a> covers each of those screens.</p>
</li>
</ol>

<h3 id="script">What the script looks like</h3>

<p>You do not need to read code for this to work, but it helps to recognise the shape so you can spot a broken one. A working script is one function that creates the form, then adds one item per question:</p>

<pre><code>function createBakeryFeedbackForm() {
  const form = FormApp.create('Bakery Customer Feedback Form');

  form.addMultipleChoiceItem()
    .setTitle('How would you rate the overall quality of our baked goods?')
    .setChoiceValues(['Excellent', 'Good', 'Average', 'Poor'])
    .setRequired(true);

  form.addCheckboxItem()
    .setTitle('Which items did you buy today?')
    .setChoiceValues(['Bread', 'Pastries', 'Cakes', 'Cookies']);

  form.addParagraphTextItem()
    .setTitle('What is one thing we could do to improve?');

  Logger.log('Form edit URL: ' + form.getEditUrl());
}</code></pre>

<p>Each item type maps to a question type in Forms: <code>addMultipleChoiceItem</code> is Multiple choice, <code>addCheckboxItem</code> is Checkboxes, <code>addListItem</code> is Dropdown, <code>addScaleItem</code> is Linear scale, <code>addTextItem</code> is Short answer and <code>addParagraphTextItem</code> is Paragraph. The <a href="https://developers.google.com/apps-script/reference/forms/form-app" target="_blank" rel="noopener">FormApp reference</a> lists the rest.</p>

<h2 id="addon">Route 3: a ChatGPT add-on inside Google Forms</h2>

<p>The Google Workspace Marketplace has several add-ons that put an AI panel in the Forms sidebar, with names like GPT for Google Forms, Magic ChatGPT Form Build and AI Quiz Maker for Google Forms. You install one, open a form, type a prompt in the sidebar and the questions appear in the editor.</p>

<p>The trade is convenience for access. Every add-on asks for permission to see and edit your Google Forms, and often your Drive as well. It runs on the developer's servers rather than Google's or OpenAI's. Most give a handful of free generations, then charge.</p>

<p>If that is acceptable, an add-on beats the script for anyone who will build forms this way every week, because there is nothing to paste and rerun.</p>

<h2 id="prompt">The prompt that gets usable questions</h2>

<p>The difference between a form you keep and a form you rebuild is almost always the prompt. ChatGPT fills in whatever you leave out, and it guesses generic. Give it these five things:</p>

<pre><code>Write the questions for a Google Form.
Purpose: customer feedback for a small bakery, after a purchase.
Audience: walk-in customers, answering on a phone in under two minutes.
Length: 8 questions.
Types: mostly Multiple choice and Linear scale (1 to 5), one Checkboxes question, one open Paragraph question at the end.
For each question, give the question text, the type, the options and whether it is required.</code></pre>

<p>Three fixes cover most of what goes wrong afterwards. If ChatGPT gives a Short answer where a Multiple choice belongs, tell it the answer must be one of a fixed list.</p>

<p>If the script errors on run, paste the exact error message back into the chat and ask for the corrected script. If nothing is required, ask it to add <code>setRequired(true)</code> to every question except the open one.</p>

<h2 id="limits">What none of the three routes can do</h2>

<ul>
<li><strong>Take payments:</strong> Google Forms has no payment field, so no AI can add one. <a href="/blog/how-to-add-payment-option-in-google-forms/">Adding a payment option to Google Forms</a> covers the workarounds.</li>
<li><strong>Add images or branding:</strong> the AI writes text. Header images, question images and the theme are still your job in the editor.</li>
<li><strong>Build sections with Gemini:</strong> Help me create a form produces one page, so a long form still needs sections added by hand.</li>
<li><strong>Edit a form later from the chat:</strong> a change in ChatGPT means a new script run, which creates a new form, or making the change yourself in Forms.</li>
<li><strong>Guarantee the question types:</strong> every route picks types from your wording, and every route sometimes picks wrong. Check the form before you publish it.</li>
</ul>

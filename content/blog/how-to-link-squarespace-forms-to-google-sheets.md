<p class="fmstr-blog-lead">Squarespace form submissions land in two places out of the box: the email inbox you nominated, and the Form & Pop-Up Storage panel inside Squarespace itself. Neither is a database.</p>

<p class="fmstr-blog-lead">You cannot filter, cannot pivot, and cannot hand the data to a teammate without exporting a CSV by hand every Monday. Google Sheets fixes that. One row per submission, live, sortable, shareable, ready for Looker Studio or a CRM import.</p>

<p class="fmstr-blog-lead">The catch: Squarespace's native Google Drive connection is famously brittle, and <a href="https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/28568908060301-Reconnecting-forms-to-Google-Drive">Squarespace itself maintains a help article on reconnecting a form when the sync silently stops</a>. If you have ever opened a Sheet to find it three weeks behind, you have met the problem.</p>

<p class="fmstr-blog-lead">This guide covers five working methods, from Squarespace's native Drive sync to a Zapier automation to a Formester embed that skips Squarespace forms entirely. Each method shows the setup, the free-tier limit, what breaks, and who it suits.</p>

<section class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps" aria-labelledby="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps-h2">
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__container">
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__head">
<h2 class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__h2" id="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps-h2">Step-by-step setup for each method</h2>
<p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__intro">Pick the method that fits your volume and budget. Each walkthrough lists the setup, pricing, and what tends to break.</p>
</div>

<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__method">
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__method-head">
<span class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__chip">Method 1</span>
<h3 class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__h3">Squarespace's native Google Drive sync</h3>
</div>
<p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__lede">Squarespace ships a built-in Google Drive connection that creates a Sheet per form and writes one row per submission. Fastest path, costs nothing, and breaks more often than any other method on this list.</p>
<p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__meta"><span><strong>Plan requirement:</strong> <a href="https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/205814638-Managing-form-and-newsletter-storage">available on every paid Squarespace plan</a>. Trial does not include form storage to Drive.</span></p>
<ol class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__list">
<li class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__item"><span class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__num" aria-hidden="true">1</span><div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__main"><p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__body">Open the page that holds your form. Hover the form block and click the pencil icon.</p></div></li>
<li class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__item"><span class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__num" aria-hidden="true">2</span><div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__main"><p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__body">Click <strong>Storage</strong> in the form editor.</p></div></li>
<li class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__item"><span class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__num" aria-hidden="true">3</span><div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__main"><p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__body">Click <strong>Add Storage</strong>, then <strong>Google Drive</strong>.</p></div></li>
<li class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__item"><span class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__num" aria-hidden="true">4</span><div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__main"><p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__body">Sign in to the Google account that should own the Sheet. Approve the Drive permission.</p></div></li>
<li class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__item"><span class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__num" aria-hidden="true">5</span><div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__main"><p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__body">Name the spreadsheet. Squarespace creates a new file in the connected Drive root folder, one Sheet per form.</p></div></li>
<li class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__item"><span class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__num" aria-hidden="true">6</span><div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__main"><p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__body">Save the form. Submit a test entry. A row should appear in the Sheet within 30 to 60 seconds.</p></div></li>
</ol>
<p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__note"><strong>What breaks:</strong> token refreshes drop and forms quietly stop writing without raising an error. Squarespace maintains a <a href="https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/28568908060301-Reconnecting-forms-to-Google-Drive">reconnect help article</a> for exactly this. Check the Sheet weekly. If a workflow depends on this sync, pick a different method.</p>
</div>

<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__method">
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__method-head">
<span class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__chip">Method 2</span>
<h3 class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__h3">Zapier (Squarespace Forms to Google Sheets)</h3>
</div>
<p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__lede">Zapier owns the integration directory entry for <a href="https://zapier.com/apps/google-sheets/integrations/squarespace-forms">Squarespace Forms and Google Sheets</a> and it is the path most Squarespace docs default to once the native Drive sync fails.</p>
<p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__meta"><span><strong>Pricing:</strong> <a href="https://zapier.com/pricing">free for 100 tasks per month</a>. One submission to Sheets is one task. Paid plans start at $19.99 per month for 750 tasks.</span></p>
<ol class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__list">
<li class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__item"><span class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__num" aria-hidden="true">1</span><div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__main"><p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__body">In Zapier, click <strong>Create Zap</strong>.</p></div></li>
<li class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__item"><span class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__num" aria-hidden="true">2</span><div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__main"><p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__body"><strong>Trigger:</strong> search Squarespace, pick <strong>New Form Submission</strong>. Connect your Squarespace account, select the site and the specific form.</p></div></li>
<li class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__item"><span class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__num" aria-hidden="true">3</span><div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__main"><p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__body">Click <strong>Test trigger</strong>. Submit a test entry in Squarespace if Zapier needs one to pull a sample.</p></div></li>
<li class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__item"><span class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__num" aria-hidden="true">4</span><div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__main"><p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__body"><strong>Action:</strong> search Google Sheets, pick <strong>Create Spreadsheet Row</strong>.</p></div></li>
<li class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__item"><span class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__num" aria-hidden="true">5</span><div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__main"><p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__body">Connect the Google account, pick the Spreadsheet and Worksheet. Map each Squarespace field to the matching column header.</p></div></li>
<li class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__item"><span class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__num" aria-hidden="true">6</span><div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__main"><p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__body"><strong>Test action.</strong> Confirm a row appears in the Sheet, then turn the Zap on.</p></div></li>
</ol>
<p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__note"><strong>What breaks:</strong> the free 100-task ceiling is the most common pain. A site doing 5 form fills a day hits it on day 20. Field mapping also resets if you rename a Squarespace form field, so re-check the Zap after any form edit.</p>
</div>

<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__method">
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__method-head">
<span class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__chip">Method 3</span>
<h3 class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__h3">Make (cheaper than Zapier at volume)</h3>
</div>
<p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__lede"><a href="https://www.make.com/en/integrations/squarespace/google-sheets">Make</a> is the Zapier alternative most Squarespace power users land on once monthly task counts climb. The free tier is 10x larger and the per-op cost is meaningfully lower at scale.</p>
<p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__meta"><span><strong>Pricing:</strong> <a href="https://www.make.com/en/pricing">1,000 operations per month free</a>. One submission written to Sheets is roughly two ops (trigger plus action). Paid starts at $10.59 per month for 10,000 ops.</span></p>
<ol class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__list">
<li class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__item"><span class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__num" aria-hidden="true">1</span><div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__main"><p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__body">In Make, click <strong>Create a new scenario</strong>.</p></div></li>
<li class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__item"><span class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__num" aria-hidden="true">2</span><div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__main"><p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__body">Add a <strong>Squarespace, Watch Form Responses</strong> module. Authorize Squarespace, pick the form.</p></div></li>
<li class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__item"><span class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__num" aria-hidden="true">3</span><div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__main"><p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__body">Set the polling interval. The free tier polls every 15 minutes, paid plans drop to 1 minute.</p></div></li>
<li class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__item"><span class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__num" aria-hidden="true">4</span><div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__main"><p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__body">Add a <strong>Google Sheets, Add a Row</strong> module. Authorize Google, pick the Sheet and worksheet.</p></div></li>
<li class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__item"><span class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__num" aria-hidden="true">5</span><div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__main"><p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__body">Map each Squarespace field to the right column. Make's mapping panel is more granular than Zapier's, useful when one form field needs to land in two columns.</p></div></li>
<li class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__item"><span class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__num" aria-hidden="true">6</span><div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__main"><p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__body">Run once with a test submission. Save and switch the scenario on.</p></div></li>
</ol>
<p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__note"><strong>What breaks:</strong> free tier polls every 15 minutes, fine for daily lead capture but useless for real-time alerts. Upgrade or use a webhook-based method if speed matters.</p>
</div>

<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__method">
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__method-head">
<span class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__chip">Method 4</span>
<h3 class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__h3">Sheet Monkey or LeadToSheet (direct webhook)</h3>
</div>
<p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__lede"><a href="https://sheetmonkey.io/integrations/how-to-submit-squarespace-forms-to-google-sheets">Sheet Monkey</a> and <a href="https://www.leadtosheet.com/integrations/squarespace">LeadToSheet</a> pipe form data straight into Sheets without an automation platform sitting in the middle. Cheaper, faster, fewer moving parts.</p>
<p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__meta"><span><strong>Pricing:</strong> Sheet Monkey is free up to 100 submissions per month, $9 per month after. LeadToSheet has a free tier with a 50-submission cap.</span></p>
<ol class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__list">
<li class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__item"><span class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__num" aria-hidden="true">1</span><div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__main"><p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__body">Create an account on Sheet Monkey or LeadToSheet. Connect Google and pick the destination Sheet.</p></div></li>
<li class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__item"><span class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__num" aria-hidden="true">2</span><div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__main"><p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__body">The tool generates a unique webhook URL.</p></div></li>
<li class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__item"><span class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__num" aria-hidden="true">3</span><div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__main"><p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__body">Squarespace cannot natively post to a custom webhook from a default Form Block. Two workarounds: use Squarespace's <strong>Code Block</strong> to inject a custom HTML form that posts to the webhook, or use <strong>Zapier Webhooks</strong> as a relay (defeats the point unless you are already on Zapier).</p></div></li>
<li class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__item"><span class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__num" aria-hidden="true">4</span><div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__main"><p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__body">Submit a test. Confirm the row.</p></div></li>
</ol>
<p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__note"><strong>What breaks:</strong> the Squarespace Form Block does not expose a custom action URL, so you are forced into a Code Block or a Zapier relay. That removes a chunk of the value. Worth it only if you cannot stomach Zapier's task pricing and you are comfortable pasting an HTML form into a code block.</p>
</div>

<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__method">
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__method-head">
<span class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__chip">Method 5</span>
<h3 class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__h3">Skip Squarespace Forms, embed a Formester form</h3>
</div>
<p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__lede">Every method above starts from the same broken assumption: that Squarespace's form block is the right collection layer. Cleaner pattern: embed a <a href="https://formester.com/">Formester</a> form inside a Squarespace Code Block and use Formester's native Google Sheets integration. The form still lives on your Squarespace page. The submission flow no longer touches Squarespace's storage layer.</p>
<p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__meta"><span><strong>Free tier:</strong> <a href="https://formester.com/pricing/">Unlimited forms and responses</a>, Sheets integration included on every plan. Paid starts at $13 per month.</span></p>
<ol class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__list">
<li class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__item"><span class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__num" aria-hidden="true">1</span><div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__main"><p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__body">Build the form in Formester. Pick the Google Sheets integration in the <strong>Automate</strong> tab, connect the Google account, pick the Sheet.</p></div></li>
<li class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__item"><span class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__num" aria-hidden="true">2</span><div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__main"><p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__body">In Formester, open <strong>Share, Embed</strong>. Copy the embed code (toggle auto-height off if you want a fixed frame).</p></div></li>
<li class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__item"><span class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__num" aria-hidden="true">3</span><div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__main"><p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__body">In Squarespace, open the page. Click <strong>Add Section</strong>, choose a blank section, click <strong>Add Block</strong>, pick <strong>Code</strong>.</p></div></li>
<li class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__item"><span class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__num" aria-hidden="true">4</span><div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__main"><p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__body">Paste the Formester embed code into the Code Block. Save.</p></div></li>
<li class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__item"><span class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__num" aria-hidden="true">5</span><div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__main"><p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__body">Submit a test. The row appears in Sheets within seconds. See the cousin guide on <a href="https://formester.com/blog/how-to-send-contact-form-data-to-google-sheets-free/">how to send contact form data to Google Sheets free</a> for the inverse setup.</p></div></li>
</ol>
<p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-steps__note"><strong>What breaks:</strong> nothing on Squarespace's side, since the form runs on Formester's infrastructure. Trade-off is the Code Block: Squarespace restricts Code Blocks on the Personal plan, so you may need the Business plan or above on Squarespace's side.</p>
</div>

<h2 class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__h2" id="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq-h2">Frequently asked questions</h2>
<p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__intro">Common questions about sending Squarespace form responses to Google Sheets.</p>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__list">

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>Does Squarespace have a native Google Sheets integration?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">Sort of. Squarespace writes form submissions to a Google Drive folder, which it creates as a Google Sheet. It is not a true integration in the Zapier sense, it is a one-way export to Drive. <a href="https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/205814638-Managing-form-and-newsletter-storage">Squarespace documents the setup and the reconnect process</a> on its help center.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>Why are my Squarespace form responses not showing up in Google Sheets?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">The most common cause is a dropped OAuth token between Squarespace and Google. Squarespace has a <a href="https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/28568908060301-Reconnecting-forms-to-Google-Drive">dedicated reconnect article</a> for this. Open the form block, go to Storage, disconnect Google Drive, and reconnect. New submissions should resume writing to the Sheet. Past submissions during the outage are lost unless they were also emailed.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>Can I use Zapier free for Squarespace to Google Sheets?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">Yes, up to <a href="https://zapier.com/pricing">100 tasks per month</a>. One submission written to Sheets equals one task. Past 100 in a month, Zapier pauses the Zap until the cycle resets or you upgrade.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>Does Make or Zapier support two-way sync?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">No. Both push form submissions one way, Squarespace to Sheets. A change made in the Sheet does not flow back to Squarespace. Two-way sync is only available with a form layer that ships its own Sheets integration, which is the Formester path in Method 5.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>Can I send Squarespace form responses to more than one Google Sheet?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">Not natively. The Drive sync writes to one Sheet per form. Use Zapier or Make to fan out a single submission into two Sheets (add a second Google Sheets action in the same Zap or scenario). On the Formester path, duplicate the integration with a second Google Sheet target.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>What happens when I add or rename a field on the form?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">On Squarespace's native Drive sync, the Sheet gains a column for the new field but old data stays in the original layout. On Zapier and Make, you need to re-open the Zap or scenario and remap the renamed field. On Formester, field changes propagate to the connected Sheet automatically as long as column headers exist.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>Is there a way to do this without Zapier?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">Yes. Use Squarespace's native Drive sync (Method 1), Make (Method 3), a direct webhook tool like Sheet Monkey (Method 4), or skip Squarespace forms and use Formester's native Sheets integration (Method 5). None of those four require a Zapier account. Embedding a Google Form in Squarespace instead? See <a href="https://formester.com/blog/how-to-embed-a-google-form/">how to embed a Google Form</a>.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>Which method is fastest to set up?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">Squarespace's native Drive sync, about 5 minutes if your Google account is already signed in. The Formester embed is the fastest method that survives Squarespace's reconnect bug, around 12 minutes end to end.</div>
</details>

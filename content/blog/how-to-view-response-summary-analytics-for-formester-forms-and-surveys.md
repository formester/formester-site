Have you ever sent out a **[form](/blog/what-is-a-form-lets-find-out/)** or survey but struggled to understand the responses? **[Without proper insights](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/why-insight-matters-more-than-ever-sharlene-zeederberg)**, your data is just numbers. 

**[Summary analytics](/features/summary-analytics/)** helps you turn responses into meaningful information, allowing you to make data-driven decisions and improve engagement.

In this post, we'll cover how to access **summary analytics** in Formester, the best ways to **analyze responses**, and use insights to improve your decision-making.

## How to Access Summary Analytics in Formester
**Step 1: Log into Your Formester Account**

**[Sign in to your Formester account](https://app.formester.com/users/sign_in)**. Once you're in, you'll see the dashboard where all your forms and surveys are listed.

**Step 2: Open the Survey You Want to Analyze**

<iframe src="![a gif on opening a quiz in formester](https://formester-strapi.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/a_gif_on_opening_a_quiz_in_formester_7947e240d9.gif)" height="300" width="100%"></iframe>

Find the form or survey for which you want insights. Click on the form’s name to open it. This will take you to the editor, where you can **view responses** and settings. If you don’t have one, click on New form to create one.

You can start from scratch or use the **[AI form generator](/ai-form-generator/)** to generate forms and surveys with a simple prompt.

**Step 3: Navigate to the Results Tab**

<iframe src="![a gif on opening response summary in formester](https://formester-strapi.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/a_gif_on_opening_response_summary_in_formester_9c05ff5449.gif)" height="300" width="100%"></iframe>


To see the summary analytics, click on the **Results** tab, then head over to **Summary**. This will display an overview of all collected responses in an easy-to-read format, helping you with survey results analysis.

### Supported Question Types for Analytics
Formester’s summary analytics work best with:

- Single-choice questions (e.g., Yes/No, Multiple Choice)

- Multiple-choice questions

- Ranking questions

- Rating scale questions

These formats provide structured data that can be summarized effectively. If you need to analyze quiz responses or conduct questionnaire data analysis, these question types offer clear insights.

### Interpreting Analytics Data

- **Choice-Based Questions:** Formester shows response percentages, helping you identify popular choices.

- **Rating-Based Questions:** View average ratings and distribution to gauge customer satisfaction.

- **Skipped Questions:** See which questions are often left unanswered to improve clarity.

- **Analyzing Open-Ended Questions:** While summary analytics focus on structured data, you can manually review text responses to uncover patterns.

### How to Make Use of Response Summary
**Identify Trends & Popular Choices**

Use survey data analysis to see which options are most popular. This helps with decision-making in marketing, product design, and customer experience.

**Fix Unclear or Skipped Questions**

If many users skip a question, it may be confusing. Try rewording it or making it optional to increase response rates.

**Segment Your Audience**

By analyzing questionnaire responses, you can group users based on preferences. This helps with targeted marketing and personalized engagement.

### Make Data-Driven Decisions

- **Improve Form Engagement:** Adjust wording and structure to get more responses.

- **Improve Business Strategies:** Use survey insights to guide marketing and product decisions.

- **Create User-Friendly Forms:** Simplify questions and optimize the design for a better experience.

### Conclusion
Response Summary analytics will give you the power to understand and transform raw responses into actionable insights, helping you make **<a href="http://defiat.io/">data-driven decisions</a>**.

Instead of guessing what works, you get clear data to improve your forms, increase engagement, and make smarter business decisions.

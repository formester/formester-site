Manually adding leads to your CRM wastes time and increases the risk of losing valuable contacts. If you want to grow your business, you need your forms and CRM working together.

With **[Formester’s HubSpot integration](/integrations/hubspot/)**, that happens automatically. Every form response, contact detail, and lead gets sent straight to your HubSpot account. No spreadsheets, no manual data entry, no missed opportunities.

## Why Connect Formester with HubSpot  

If you are using HubSpot to manage your contacts and Formester to collect leads, keeping them connected just makes sense.

**Here is what you gain:**

**Instant Lead Capture**
As soon as someone fills out your form, their information flows straight into HubSpot. No copy-pasting, no delays.  

**Complete Control Over Data** 
You decide which form fields go into your CRM. Send only what matters. Keep your HubSpot account clean and easy to manage.  

**Never Miss a Lead** 
Every response gets captured automatically, even incomplete ones. That means no lost contacts, no missed follow-ups.  

**Simpler Workflows**  
Your marketing and sales teams always have up-to-date information without extra effort.  

### Step by Step: How to Connect Formester with HubSpot  

Getting started takes just a few minutes. You do not need any technical skills. Here is how to set it up:  

**1. Choose Your Form**  
Log in to Formester and select the form you want to connect to HubSpot.  

**2. Open the Automate Tab**  
Inside your form settings, go to the Automate section. You will see a list of available integrations.  

**3. Enable the HubSpot Integration**  
Find HubSpot in the list and turn it ON.  

**4. Connect Your HubSpot Account**  
Sign in to your HubSpot account and authorize the connection. It is quick, secure, and only needs to be done once.  

**5. Map Your Form Fields**  
Match the fields in your Formester form to the correct HubSpot properties. You control what gets sent and how it is stored.  

**6. Publish Your Form**  
Share your form anywhere on your website, landing pages, or emails. Every response goes straight into HubSpot automatically.

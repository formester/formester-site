**Filling out a PDF document** by hand takes time. Copy-pasting information into a PDF file is tedious and often leads to mistakes. Chasing people for electronic signatures over email only slows everything down.

There is a better way to handle this entire signing process.

With the right form, you can collect information, **auto fill that data into a PDF**, and get the document signed. You can even sign PDFs online, without printing, scanning, or extra tools.

All of this happens automatically in one smooth process.

This guide will show you how to do it quickly and easily, even if you have no technical background.

## Why Use a Form to Auto-Fill and Sign PDFs?
Manually filling and signing PDFs takes time. So does chasing people for digital signatures or asking them to download, sign, and send back a signed document.

With an **automated form and PDF workflow**, you can:

- Save hours of repetitive work

- Eliminate manual copy-pasting

- Avoid formatting errors and lost files

- Collect legally binding **pdf signatures**

- Get polished, ready-to-send documents instantly

- Sign the document online without hassle

- Reduce dependency on tools like **Adobe Cloud Storage** just for basic signing needs

It is simple, professional, and completely online.

### Auto-Fill and Sign PDFs with Formester
Here is how you can fill and sign your PDFs automatically with Formester.

### Step 1. Create Your Form with an E Signature Field

![a screenshot of formester's form builder](https://formester-strapi.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/a_screenshot_of_formester_s_form_builder_bb593ad594.png)


- Start by building your form inside Formester.

- Add the form fields you need like Name, email address, or Address.

- Make sure to include an **[E Signature field](/features/electronic-signature/)** so users can add their signature directly when filling the form. They can even draw your signature using a mouse or touch screen.

This is how the system automatically captures both the details and the signature or initials.

### Step 2. Upload Your Blank PDF

![a screenshot of formester's pdf editor](https://formester-strapi.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/a_screenshot_of_formester_s_pdf_editor_cb21bfc24b.png)

- Once your form is ready, head to the Advanced tab and select **[Connect to PDF](/features/create-fillable-pdf-forms/)**.

- Upload your pdf document. This could be a contract, agreement, application, or any document you want to fill and sign.

- This is the file that will get auto filled with your form fields and ready for signature.

### Step 3. Add Text Fields and Use the @ Sign to Map Form Responses
![a screenshot of formester's pdf editor](https://formester-strapi.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/a_screenshot_of_formester_s_pdf_editor_ab15b9a684.png)

- Place text fields on your PDF where the information should appear.

- Use the @ sign to connect each field from the form to your pdf file.

**For example:**

- @Name pulls in the person’s name.

- @Email adds their email address.

Now, when someone fills the form, their details appear in the correct spots on the pdf document.

### Step 4. Add the Signature with the @ Sign
![a screenshot of formester's pdf editor](https://formester-strapi.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/a_screenshot_of_formester_s_pdf_editor_977e0cca56.png)

- Finally, sign the document setup.

- Place an image element on the PDF where the signature or initials should appear.

- Use the **@ sign** to pull in the electronic signatures directly from the form.

Your PDF now auto fills with the details and includes a legally binding signature.

### Final Step. Test and Send

- Before sharing your form, do a quick test submission.

- Check how the filled and signed document looks.

- Once you are happy with everything, send the form link to anyone who needs to complete and sign the document.

The process is fully automated. As soon as someone submits the form, you get a **ready-to-use**, signed PDF instantly.

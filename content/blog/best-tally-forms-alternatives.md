## Why Look for a Tally.so Alternative?

Tally is great for beginners, but here is why people switch:

- **You hit limits with the free version**  
The basics are fine, but paid features sneak in fast.

- **You need advanced customization**  
Tally lets you tweak a little, but adding your own style or custom CSS is limited.

- **You want more form fields and smarter logic**  
Complex forms often need more than what Tally provides.

- **You care about branding with a custom domain**  
Your forms should match your website, not look like someone else’s product.

- **You need better data collection tools**  
Smooth connections to tools like Google Sheets help manage responses better.

- **You are ready for more than basic paid plans**  
Tally’s pro plan is simple, but growing teams often need more.

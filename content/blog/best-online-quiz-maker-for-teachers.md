### Traditional Tests vs. Online Quizzes
Traditional tests take more time to prepare and grade. They’re also less interactive. Online quizzes, on the other hand, are:

* Faster to create and evaluate.

* More engaging with multimedia support.

* Easier to share and scale.

* Eco-friendly, as they reduce paper use.

<h2 class="fmstr-cmp-bmf-faq__h2" id="fmstr-cmp-bmf-faq-h2">Frequently asked questions</h2>
<p class="fmstr-cmp-bmf-faq__intro">Common questions about mobile form builders, native vs web, offline, and pricing.</p>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bmf-faq__list">

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bmf-faq__item"><summary>What is a mobile form builder?</summary><div class="fmstr-cmp-bmf-faq__answer">A mobile form builder creates forms designed to work well on phones and tablets. Some are native mobile apps (Jotform Mobile, Zonka Feedback); others are mobile-responsive web builders (Formester, Tally, Typeform).</div></details>
<details class="fmstr-cmp-bmf-faq__item"><summary>Native app or mobile-responsive web, which is better?</summary><div class="fmstr-cmp-bmf-faq__answer">For daily field use, native apps are smoother (offline, push, tap targets). For one-off submissions, mobile-responsive web is cheaper and reaches more people without an install.</div></details>
<details class="fmstr-cmp-bmf-faq__item"><summary>Do mobile form builders work offline?</summary><div class="fmstr-cmp-bmf-faq__answer">Native apps (Jotform Mobile, Zonka Feedback) handle offline natively. Among web-based, Formester is the standout, forms cache in the browser and submissions sync on reconnect.</div></details>
<details class="fmstr-cmp-bmf-faq__item"><summary>Which mobile form builder is cheapest?</summary><div class="fmstr-cmp-bmf-faq__answer">Tally has the most generous free plan (unlimited). Formester is cheapest paid at $11/mo. Jotform Mobile starts at $34/mo for paid.</div></details>
<details class="fmstr-cmp-bmf-faq__item"><summary>Are mobile forms secure?</summary><div class="fmstr-cmp-bmf-faq__answer">Yes if you choose HTTPS-only, GDPR / CCPA compliant, AES-256 at rest. Formester is GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA; Jotform offers HIPAA on Gold+.</div></details>
<details class="fmstr-cmp-bmf-faq__item"><summary>Can mobile forms accept payments?</summary><div class="fmstr-cmp-bmf-faq__answer">Yes. Formester, Jotform Mobile, forms.app, Typeform, Tally, Paperform, Fillout, and Zoho support mobile payments via Stripe / PayPal / Square.</div></details>
<details class="fmstr-cmp-bmf-faq__item"><summary>Photo upload and signature?</summary><div class="fmstr-cmp-bmf-faq__answer">Yes for all 10 tools. Photos compress on-device and upload with the form. Signatures use the device touch screen.</div></details>
<details class="fmstr-cmp-bmf-faq__item"><summary>Mobile form builder for kiosks?</summary><div class="fmstr-cmp-bmf-faq__answer">Formester (full-screen embed), Jotform Mobile (built-in kiosk mode), Zonka Feedback (purpose-built kiosks) handle this well.</div></details>
<details class="fmstr-cmp-bmf-faq__item"><summary>Best for AI?</summary><div class="fmstr-cmp-bmf-faq__answer">forms.app leads with AI-first positioning. Formester, Jotform, and Typeform all have AI form generators with comparable output.</div></details>
<details class="fmstr-cmp-bmf-faq__item"><summary>Embed in mobile app or PWA?</summary><div class="fmstr-cmp-bmf-faq__answer">Yes. Most tools provide iframe + JavaScript embed that works inside a WebView. Formester's six embed modes give the most flexibility.</div></details>

A good onboarding process can **[make or break a new hire's experience](https://unboxedtechnology.com/blog/importance-of-new-employee-onboarding-process/)**. It’s your chance to help them settle in, understand their role, and feel like part of the team. But how do you know if your onboarding is effective?

That’s where **new hire onboarding surveys** come in! These **[surveys](/templates/categories/survey-forms/)** give you honest feedback to fine-tune your process, making it smooth and enjoyable for every employee. 

In this blog, you’ll get ready-to-use survey questions and learn how to create a survey effortlessly.
### Why Use New Hire Onboarding Surveys?
**1. Make a Great First Impression**
When you ask for **feedback**, it shows you care about your employees’ experiences. This can help build trust and make them feel valued right from the start.

**2. Spot Problem Areas**
**[Surveys can uncover gaps](/blog/5-things-you-can-do-with-survey-forms/)** in your onboarding process. Maybe the training wasn’t clear, or some tools weren’t accessible. Fixing these issues improves the experience for future hires.

**3. Increase Engagement and Retention**
When employees feel supported, they’re more likely to stay and perform well. Happy hires lead to a stronger, more motivated team!

### Best Practices for Onboarding Surveys
Below are the best practices you should follow while creating your onboarding surveys:
**1). Keep It Simple:**
A short, focused survey is easier for employees to complete. **[Stick to 10–15 well-thought-out questions](/blog/best-survey-questions/)** to ensure higher response rates.

**2). Mix Question Types:**
Combine yes/no questions, rating scales, and open-ended responses to gather diverse and detailed feedback. Each question type serves a unique purpose, so variety is key.

**3). Send Surveys at the Right Time:**
Timing is everything. For fresh insights, send the survey during their first week. To get deeper feedback, wait until the **30-day mark** once they’ve settled in.

**4). Take Action:**
Don’t just collect feedback, act on it. Let your employees see that their suggestions are valued and lead to real improvements. This builds trust and fosters a culture of openness.

### Top New Hire Onboarding Survey Questions
To help you get started, here are some great questions divided into helpful categories:

**Pre-Onboarding Experience:**

1). Did you get all the information you needed before your first day?

2). Was the onboarding schedule shared with you ahead of time?

**First Day Experience:**

3). How would you describe your first day? (Open-ended)

4). Did you feel welcomed by your manager and team?

**Clarity of Role and Responsibilities:**

5). Do you clearly understand your job responsibilities?

6). Is your manager supporting you effectively?

**Tools and Resources:**

7). Were all necessary tools and systems provided to you on time?

8). Did you receive enough training to use company tools/software?

**Overall Satisfaction:**

9). On a scale of 1–10, how would you rate your onboarding experience?

10). What can we improve in the onboarding process for future hires? (Open-ended)

<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__container">
<h2 class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__h2" id="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq-h2">Frequently asked questions</h2>
<p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__intro">Common questions about converting any PDF, scanned or digital, into a fillable form.</p>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__list">

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>Is there a free way to convert a PDF to a fillable form?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">Yes. Formester's free plan covers the full AI conversion flow (upload, auto-detect, publish) with files up to 100MB. PDF24, PDFgear, and the Sejda free tier (3 tasks per hour) also work for one-off jobs. Adobe Acrobat is paid only.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>Can I convert a scanned paper PDF, not just a digital one?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">Yes. Formester, Adobe Acrobat, Sejda, Smallpdf, iLovePDF, PDF24, and PDFgear all run OCR on scanned PDFs. Only Formester and Acrobat auto-detect fields after OCR; the rest expect you to draw fields manually.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>Does the converted form keep the original PDF format?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">Two answers depending on the route. The AI route publishes a web form; the original PDF is the template, the output is a shareable link. The PDF Editor route (or the Adobe Acrobat route) preserves the PDF format: every submission produces a filled, downloadable PDF that looks identical to the original.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>How do I add e-signatures?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">Drop a signature field anywhere on the form. Respondents sign with a finger on mobile, a mouse on desktop, or by typing their name in a script font. The signature is captured with timestamp and IP for audit. Available on the <a href="https://formester.com/features/electronic-signature/">Formester signature feature</a>.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>Can I send the filled PDF back to the respondent automatically?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">Yes on Formester (turn on auto-send in form settings; Business plan and above for the auto-PDF feature). Adobe Acrobat with Acrobat Sign also does this. Free online tools generally do not. You can also <a href="https://formester.com/features/export-form-submission-pdf/">export submissions as PDF</a> in bulk from the dashboard.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>What file size can I upload?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">Formester: 100MB free, 1GB Personal, 50GB Business per file. Sejda free: 50MB or 200 pages. Smallpdf free: ~5MB. Acrobat Pro: practically unlimited (limited by your machine).</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>Is the conversion accessible (WCAG / 508)?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">Web forms are easier to make accessible than PDFs. Formester's web output supports keyboard navigation, screen readers, and color-contrast settings out of the box. For Acrobat's PDF route, you need to set the Tags tree manually after conversion, which is the most common gap in agency workflows.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>Can I connect submissions to Google Sheets, Slack, or my CRM?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">Yes on Formester and Jotform via native integrations. On the PDF-only tools (Sejda, PDF24, Smallpdf), you get an emailed PDF; routing to a CRM requires Zapier or manual upload.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>Does the AI detect every field, every time?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">No tool does. Clean digital PDFs hit close to 95-100% on basic fields. Scanned phone photos with shadows or skew drop to 70-85%. The fix is always the same: review the detected fields once, fix any miss, save the template, and the next 100 conversions of the same form are perfect.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>Can I make a fillable PDF from a Word document?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">Yes. Export the Word doc as a PDF first (File, Save As, PDF), then run it through any of the three methods in this guide. Formester also accepts the Word file directly.</div>
</details>

<h2 class="fmstr-cmp-bfst-wdg__h2" id="fmstr-cmp-bfst-wdg-h2">When you need more than a survey tool</h2>
<p class="fmstr-cmp-bfst-wdg__p">A survey tool is built for one job: ask questions, see results, move on. Most of the above are excellent at that.</p>
<p class="fmstr-cmp-bfst-wdg__p">The picture changes when the survey is not the end of the workflow. If a "yes" on question 7 needs to trigger a different email than a "no", route the response to a different CRM stage, charge a different price, or hand off to a different team owner, you are not building a survey anymore. You are building a workflow with a survey in front of it. Pure survey tools do not handle this cleanly: they will export to Sheets and expect you to bolt on Zapier, Make, or a custom script. That is fine until the third or fourth condition, when the duct tape starts to show. The same is true if you are collecting payments alongside answers, asking for file uploads that need to land in a specific Drive folder by respondent type, or running a form that has to feel branded end-to-end on mobile. At that point a form builder with survey logic outperforms a survey tool with a payments add-on. <a href="https://formester.com/online-survey-maker/">Formester</a> is built for that hybrid case: conditional logic, payments, file uploads, signatures, Google Sheets and CRM sync, and custom branding all on the same plan, no Zapier required.</p>

<div class="fmstr-cmp-bfst-faq__container">
<h2 class="fmstr-cmp-bfst-faq__h2" id="fmstr-cmp-bfst-faq-h2">FAQ</h2>
<p class="fmstr-cmp-bfst-faq__intro">Eight quick answers on free survey tool limits, exports, logic, branding, and anonymous use.</p>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bfst-faq__list">

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bfst-faq__item">
<summary>What is the actual difference between a survey tool and a form builder?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bfst-faq__answer">A survey tool is optimized for asking questions and analyzing answers: question libraries, response analytics, cross-tabs, sentiment scoring. A form builder is optimized for collecting and routing data: payments, file uploads, conditional routing, integrations, branded experiences. The lines blur, but if your end output is a chart, you want a survey tool. If your end output is a workflow, you want a form builder.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bfst-faq__item">
<summary>Which free survey tool has no response cap?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bfst-faq__answer">Google Forms and Tally are the only two on this list with no response cap on the free plan. Google Forms has zero caps inside personal or Workspace accounts. Tally is unlimited under a fair-use policy.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bfst-faq__item">
<summary>Can I export survey responses to Google Sheets for free?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bfst-faq__answer">Google Forms, Tally, Jotform, and Microsoft Forms (via Excel) all support free exports. SurveyMonkey locks export behind the paid Standard plan. Typeform charges for native integrations.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bfst-faq__item">
<summary>Do free plans allow branching or skip logic?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bfst-faq__answer">Most do, with limits. Qualtrics XM Free includes skip, display, and branch logic. Tally and Jotform include conditional logic on free. SurveyMonkey gives basic skip logic. Google Forms supports section-based branching only. Typeform's free plan limits how many logic jumps you can set.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bfst-faq__item">
<summary>Will the free survey show vendor branding?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bfst-faq__answer">Yes on almost every paid-tier vendor: SurveyMonkey, Typeform, Tally, Jotform, Sogolytics, SurveySparrow, Qualtrics. Google Forms and Microsoft Forms only show their own product footer, which most people read as neutral.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bfst-faq__item">
<summary>Which survey tool has the best mobile completion rate?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bfst-faq__answer">Typeform's one-question-per-screen format and SurveySparrow's chat-style flow consistently lift completion on mobile, which is why both charge premium pricing for it. Tally and Formester both render mobile-first by default. Static form vendors lag.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bfst-faq__item">
<summary>Can I run an anonymous survey on the free plan?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bfst-faq__answer">Yes on Google Forms (toggle off "collect email"), Microsoft Forms (toggle off identity capture), Qualtrics XM Free, SurveyMonkey, and Tally. Check the response settings before launch; some tools default to capturing email.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bfst-faq__item">
<summary>What is the best free survey tool for academic research?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bfst-faq__answer">Qualtrics XM Free. 500 responses, skip/display/branch logic, SPSS export, no expiration, recognized by most university IRB review boards. The free account is the same product as the paid version with lower limits.</div>
</details>

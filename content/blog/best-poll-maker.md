<h2>Which poll maker should you choose?</h2>

<p>Polls split into two situations. One is live: a room, a webinar, or a lecture where results appear on screen as people answer. The other is asynchronous, where you send a link and collect votes over days.</p>

<p>Tools built for live events price by audience size, which is the number you can least predict. Tools built for async polling price by responses or not at all. Knowing which situation you are in removes half the shortlist immediately.</p>

<div class="tldr">
<p><strong>The 60-second TL;DR</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Best for live audience engagement:</strong> Mentimeter, from $14 per presenter/mo</li>

<li><strong>Best for large lecture halls:</strong> Poll Everywhere, from $10/mo</li>

<li><strong>Best for polished async polls:</strong> Typeform, from $39/mo</li>
</ul>

<h2>How to pick the right poll maker</h2>

<p>Answer the live-or-async question first, because it splits this list cleanly in two. If a room needs to see results appear while people answer, you are choosing between Mentimeter, Slido and Poll Everywhere.</p>

<p>Within that group, the tie-breaker is the shape of your event. Mentimeter suits workshops and teaching, Slido suits all-hands meetings that need Q&amp;A moderation, and Poll Everywhere suits audiences large enough that capacity is the real constraint.</p>

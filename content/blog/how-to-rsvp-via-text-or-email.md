## Why Use an RSVP Form?
An RSVP form keeps all responses in one place. You don’t have to track texts or emails manually.

It helps avoid miscommunication. Guests can confirm or decline with just one click.

Formester also sends automatic reminders, so no one forgets to reply.

If you're wondering how many people can RSVP to your Evite event, that depends on the platform. With Formester, you can collect unlimited RSVPs without restrictions.

- <strong><a href="https://app.formester.com/users/sign_in">Log in to Formester</a></strong> (or sign up if you’re new).

- Use the <strong><a href="/ai-form-generator/">AI Form Builder</a></strong> to generate an RSVP form instantly with a simple prompt such as "<strong>Create an RSVP Form For A Wedding</strong>"

- Add event details like <strong>date, location</strong>, and plus-one options.

- Customize the form with extra questions like <strong>dietary needs</strong> or special requests.

- Set up <strong><a href="/features/autoresponder-email/">automatic confirmation emails</a></strong> to inform guests that their response was received.

- Publish the form and get a shareable link to send out.

### How to RSVP via Text Using Formester
If you’re unsure how to RSVP via text, it's easy with Formester.

- Copy the form link from Formester.

- Send a short message like:
“<strong>Hey [Name], please RSVP for [Event Name] here: [Form Link]. Hope to see you there!</strong>”

Then track responses in real time using Formester’s form analytics dashboard.

### How to RSVP via Email Using Formester
If you need to know how to RSVP via email, it’s just as simple. If you don't have all the guest emails yet, an <strong><a href="https://getprospect.com/">email finder tool</a></strong> can help locate them quickly.

- Write a short and clear email.

- Use a subject like “<strong>RSVP for [Event Name] – Confirm Your Attendance</strong>”.

- Here’s a simple message you can send:
“<strong>We’re excited to invite you to [Event Name]! Please RSVP by [Date] using this link: [Form Link]. Let us know if you have any questions!</strong>”

### Best Practices for RSVPs
- Send your RSVP request early so guests have time to respond.
- Follow up with reminders.
- Keep your form simple and quick to fill out.

<h2 class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__h2" id="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq-h2">Frequently asked questions</h2>
<p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__intro">Common questions about setting up RSVP collection over text or email.</p>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__list">

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>How do I RSVP by text message?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">Send a text invitation that includes a short link to an online RSVP form. The guest taps the link, fills in attending yes/no and any extra info (dietary preferences, plus-one), and submits. You see every response in a dashboard.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>How do I set up text-message RSVPs for an event?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">Build an RSVP form in Formester or any form builder, copy the short link, then send the link in a bulk SMS through a tool like Twilio, EZ Texting, or your contacts app. Guests tap, fill, and submit.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>Can I send RSVPs by email instead of text?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">Yes. Same setup: build the form, copy the link, and paste it into your email invitation. Email RSVPs work especially well for corporate events and weddings.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>What information should an RSVP form collect?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">Guest name, attending yes/no, number of guests/plus-ones, dietary preferences, song requests (for weddings), and any allergies or accessibility needs. Keep it under 6 questions to maximize completion.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>Can I send automatic reminders to non-responders?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">Yes. Set up an autoresponder that detects non-responses after a set time (e.g., 7 days before the event) and sends a follow-up email or text. Most form builders integrate with email tools to handle this.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>How do I track RSVPs in real time?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">Open the form's response dashboard. Every submission appears immediately with a running count of yes/no, total guests, and dietary tally. Some tools also offer a public RSVP page that updates live.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>Can I cap the number of RSVPs?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">Yes. Use the form limiter feature to cap submissions at a venue capacity. Once the cap is hit, the form switches to a waitlist mode.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>Is text RSVP cheaper than printed RSVP cards?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">Yes by a wide margin. Printed RSVP cards run $1-3 per guest. Text or email RSVPs cost less than 5 cents per guest (just the SMS sending cost). For 100 guests, you save $95-295.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>Can I include a wedding RSVP form on a wedding website?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">Yes. Embed the RSVP form on your wedding website via iframe, or link to it from the wedding details page. Most wedding website platforms (Squarespace, Wix, The Knot) support form embeds.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>What's the best RSVP form for a wedding?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">A wedding RSVP form should collect guest name, attending yes/no, meal selection, plus-one details, song requests, and any allergies. Formester's wedding RSVP template ships with all of those fields pre-built.</div>
</details>

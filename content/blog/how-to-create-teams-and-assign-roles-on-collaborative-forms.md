## Inviting Members to Your Organization
When you sign up for Formester, your account automatically creates an organization. This is where you manage members and forms. Here’s how to get started:

**Step 1: Navigate to Organization Members**
Go to the Teams tab and click on Organization Members. This is where you can manage your team.

**Step 2: Invite Members & Assign Roles**
Invite people to join your organization by sending them an email invitation. When inviting, assign them one of these roles:

* **Admin:** Has full control over all forms and settings in your organization.

* **Staff:** Can only access the forms they’ve been given access to.

**Step 3: Collaborate**
Once your invitee accepts, they can access your organization by switching to it from the top-right corner of the dashboard.

### How to Create Teams and Assign Roles
If you need to collaborate with a group, the Teams feature is perfect. It lets you invite multiple people, group them into teams, and assign specific roles for better collaboration.

For example, if you want to run a company-wide survey, you can create a team called "Employees" and assign submitter access to all the employees in that team.

**Step 1: Create a Team**
Go to the Teams tab and create a new team. This allows you to manage forms and roles for the entire group.

**Step 2: Invite Members & Assign Roles**
Send email invites to your team members and assign one of these roles:

* **Maintainer:** Can manage team memberships, roles, and has full access to all team-assigned forms.

* **Member:** Can access forms assigned to the team but cannot manage members.

**Step 3: Collaborate**
Assign form-level access to the team to work together on specific forms or surveys.


### Set Up Form Level Access
Form-level access lets you control who can see and edit each individual form or survey. You can assign access to specific people or an entire team.

**Step 1: Create a Form or Survey**
Use Formester’s **[pre-built templates](/templates/)** or **[AI form generator](/ai-form-generator/)** to quickly create your form or survey.

**Step 2: Invite People & Assign Roles**
Go to the Share tab in your form and invite people by searching for their names. Assign roles based on what they need to do:

* Maintainer: Full control over the form, including managing access and editing.

* Editor: Can edit the form and view submissions but cannot manage access.

* Viewer: Can only view submissions and analytics.

* Submitter: Can only fill out and submit the form.

This way, you control exactly who sees, edits, or reviews each form or survey, ensuring secure collaboration.


### Why Use Collaborative Forms and Surveys?
Collaborative forms and surveys are essential tools for modern teamwork. Here's why you should use them:

* Clearly defined roles ensure everyone knows their responsibilities, making collaboration smooth and efficient.

* Keep all your forms and access controls in one place for better organization.

* Assigning specific roles reduces confusion and helps your team focus on their tasks.

* Grant form-level access to individuals or teams, ensuring the right people have the right permissions.

* Teams can easily collaborate on forms, share ideas, and manage feedback seamlessly.

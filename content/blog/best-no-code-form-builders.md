<div class="fmstr-cmp-bnc-faq__container">
<h2 class="fmstr-cmp-bnc-faq__h2" id="fmstr-cmp-bnc-faq-h2">Frequently asked questions</h2>
<p class="fmstr-cmp-bnc-faq__intro">Common questions about no-code form builders, pricing, AI, and integrations.</p>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bnc-faq__list">

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bnc-faq__item"><summary>What is a no-code form builder?</summary><div class="fmstr-cmp-bnc-faq__answer">A no-code form builder is a tool that lets non-developers create web forms by dragging and dropping fields (text, email, dropdown, file upload, signature) into a visual editor. No HTML, CSS, or JavaScript required. The tool handles form rendering, validation, submission storage, and integrations with email/CRM/Sheets.</div></details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bnc-faq__item"><summary>Are no-code form builders free?</summary><div class="fmstr-cmp-bnc-faq__answer">Most have a free tier. The most generous free tiers in 2026 are Tally (unlimited forms + responses) and Formester (100 responses/mo, unlimited forms). Jotform, Typeform, and Wufoo cap free tiers at 10-100 responses/month. Google Forms and Microsoft Forms are free if you're already on Google Workspace or Microsoft 365.</div></details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bnc-faq__item"><summary>What's the difference between no-code and low-code form builders?</summary><div class="fmstr-cmp-bnc-faq__answer">No-code form builders require zero code, drag fields, configure logic in a visual editor, ship. Low-code form builders let you add custom JavaScript, CSS, or webhook logic when you outgrow the visual editor. For 95% of users, no-code is enough.</div></details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bnc-faq__item"><summary>Which no-code form builder is best for AI?</summary><div class="fmstr-cmp-bnc-faq__answer">Formester, Jotform, Typeform, Paperform, and Fillout all ship AI form generators in 2026. Formester's runs on Google Gemini and accepts plain-text prompts, paste-in question lists, or PDF uploads. Jotform's AI also supports prompt + PDF. Output quality is comparable; the differentiator is whether the AI works with the rest of the platform.</div></details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bnc-faq__item"><summary>Can no-code form builders collect payments?</summary><div class="fmstr-cmp-bnc-faq__answer">Yes. Formester (Stripe + PayPal), Jotform (Stripe, PayPal, Square + 30 others), Typeform (Stripe), Tally (Stripe), Paperform (Stripe + Square + Braintree), Fillout (Stripe), Wufoo (Stripe + PayPal), and Zoho Forms (Stripe, PayPal, Authorize.net) all support payments natively. Google Forms and Microsoft Forms do not.</div></details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bnc-faq__item"><summary>Which no-code form builder has the best conditional logic?</summary><div class="fmstr-cmp-bnc-faq__answer">Formester, Jotform, Typeform, Paperform, and Fillout all handle visual conditional logic well. Typeform's flowchart editor is the most visual. Formester's logic editor handles multi-condition rules (if X AND Y → show Z) without code. Tally's logic is text-only.</div></details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bnc-faq__item"><summary>Are no-code forms secure?</summary><div class="fmstr-cmp-bnc-faq__answer">Yes, with the right tool. Look for HTTPS-only submissions, GDPR / CCPA compliance, AES-256 encryption at rest, and SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certification (for enterprise needs). Formester is GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA compliant with AES-256 on the payments layer; Jotform offers HIPAA on Gold+; Typeform and Tally are GDPR-compliant but not SOC 2 or HIPAA certified.</div></details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bnc-faq__item"><summary>Can I embed a no-code form on my website?</summary><div class="fmstr-cmp-bnc-faq__answer">Yes, every tool listed supports iframe and JavaScript embed. Formester ships six embed modes (inline, popup, sidebar, side tab, popover, fullscreen). Jotform and Typeform offer similar. Embed snippets work on WordPress, Webflow, Squarespace, Shopify, and custom HTML sites.</div></details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bnc-faq__item"><summary>How do no-code form builders compare on price-per-submission?</summary><div class="fmstr-cmp-bnc-faq__answer">At 1,000 submissions/month: Tally Pro $24 (unlimited) = $0.029. Formester Personal $13 (5,000 cap) = $0.0022. Fillout Starter $15 (1,000 cap) = $0.0038. Jotform Bronze $34 (1,000 cap) = $0.034. Typeform Plus $79 (1,000 cap) = $0.005. Formester wins on raw cost per submission at this scale; Tally wins if you're under 1,000.</div></details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bnc-faq__item"><summary>Can a no-code form builder replace a developer?</summary><div class="fmstr-cmp-bnc-faq__answer">For 95% of standard forms, yes. Lead capture, surveys, payment forms, RSVPs, internal intake are all faster to ship in Formester, Tally, or Jotform than to code from scratch. Developers become useful when you need custom validation logic, deep CRM workflows that go beyond Zapier, or pixel-perfect design on a marketing landing page.</div></details>

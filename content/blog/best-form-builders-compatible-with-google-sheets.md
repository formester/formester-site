<section class="fmstr-cmp-bfg-faq" aria-labelledby="fmstr-cmp-bfg-faq-h2">
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bfg-faq__container">
<h2 class="fmstr-cmp-bfg-faq__h2" id="fmstr-cmp-bfg-faq-h2">Frequently asked questions</h2>
<p class="fmstr-cmp-bfg-faq__intro">Common questions about Google Sheets sync, free plans, and switching between form builders.</p>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bfg-faq__list">

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bfg-faq__item">
<summary>How does a form builder actually sync with Google Sheets?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bfg-faq__answer">The clean answer is a native integration. The form builder talks to Google's Sheets API, either creating a sheet for you or appending rows to one you pick. The messier answer is Apps Script (a Google-side script that listens to submissions, common with Google Forms) or Zapier (a third-party automation that polls for new responses and pushes them to a row). Native is fastest. Zapier adds cost and a polling delay. Apps Script breaks when the API changes.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bfg-faq__item">
<summary>Which form builders sync to Google Sheets natively, without Zapier?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bfg-faq__answer">Formester, Google Forms, Jotform, Paperform, Formstack, Fillout, Zoho Forms and Tally all offer native Google Sheets connectors in 2026. Typeform and Cognito Forms still route through Zapier or Make.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bfg-faq__item">
<summary>What is two-way Google Sheets sync, and which form builders support it?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bfg-faq__answer">Two-way means the form not only writes new submissions to the sheet but also reads from the sheet to populate fields. Common uses: pulling a price list, pre-filling a registration form with member data, or building a product picker backed by a spreadsheet. <a href="https://www.fillout.com/">Fillout</a> and Formester (via auto-fill from Sheets) handle this. Most others are one-way.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bfg-faq__item">
<summary>Why are my form responses not appearing in Google Sheets?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bfg-faq__answer">Three usual suspects. First, the connection token expired (re-authorise the integration). Second, the destination sheet was renamed or moved (rebuild the link). Third, you added a new form field after the integration was set up, and the columns no longer match (most tools require you to re-map fields or re-create the sheet). On Google Forms specifically, check that the linked sheet wasn't moved to a Trash folder.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bfg-faq__item">
<summary>Is there a free form builder with a real Google Sheets integration?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bfg-faq__answer">Yes, several. Formester's free plan includes the native Sheets integration with unlimited responses. <a href="https://www.fillout.com/pricing">Fillout's free plan</a> covers 1,000 responses with native sync. Google Forms is free with unlimited responses, capped by Drive storage and the <a href="https://support.google.com/drive/answer/37603">10 million cell limit</a> in Sheets. Tally's free plan covers unlimited forms with the native connector.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bfg-faq__item">
<summary>Does the form builder build the Google Sheet for me, or do I create it first?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bfg-faq__answer">Depends on the tool. Native integrations (Formester, Google Forms, Jotform, Fillout) usually offer both: create a new sheet on connect, or pick an existing one and map fields. Zapier-based connections expect you to pre-build the sheet with column headers, then map manually.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bfg-faq__item">
<summary>What happens to my Google Sheet when I delete the form?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bfg-faq__answer">The sheet stays in your Drive. The form builder stops writing new rows, but historical responses are not erased. If you also want the data gone, delete the sheet manually.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bfg-faq__item">
<summary>How do I move my Google Forms responses to a different form builder without losing data?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bfg-faq__answer">Export the linked sheet as CSV from Sheets. Import the CSV into the new tool's submissions or response storage area (most form builders, including Formester, Jotform and Fillout, support CSV import). Rebuild the form in the new tool, connect it to a fresh Google Sheet, and archive the old one.</div>
</details>

The Cosmos Persona Quiz is a fun personality test with questions that will help you understand your cosmic personality.

As you answer 12 easy questions about what you would do in different situations, the quiz will give you special results. These results could be things like Star Seeker, Moon Explorer, or Solar Wanderer.

Did you know you can earn money just by sharing **[form builders](/)**?

Most people use these tools to collect data, run surveys, or manage signups. But few realize they can also become a steady source of income.

By joining **affiliate marketing programs**, you can suggest form builders to your audience. You will earn a commission for each signup. This makes them a great choice for content creators, bloggers, and digital marketers. They want to increase revenue from their marketing efforts.

No matter if you write a blog, make YouTube videos, or run email campaigns, these programs help you earn money. They let you share tools that your audience will really use.

They are **commissions** based and designed to reward affiliates who promote the right products and services. By tracking your **referrals**, you can boost conversion rates. You can also improve your landing page and coupon codes to increase earnings.

Here’s a detailed look at the **top form builder affiliate programs**, ranked by earning potential and long-term value.

## 1. Formester Affiliate Program
![a screenshot of formester's affiliate program](https://formester-strapi.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/a_screenshot_of_formester_s_affiliate_program_0d1d5a4825.png)

**Earnings:** Up to 30 percent recurring commission for life

**[Formester's affiliate program](/affiliate-program/)**) is one of the most rewarding options. You can earn a commission for life for every customer who signs up through your referral link. Even free users who later upgrade will continue to add to your income, helping you generate consistent passive income.

Affiliates receive a custom link and ready-to-use marketing tools. These include banners, email campaigns, templates, and social media posts. Sharing Formester on your blog post, newsletter, or social channels is easy and effective.

**Who it’s for:** Content creators, digital marketers, and agencies recommending simple no code tools.

**Why it ranks first:** Formester’s lifetime commission model lets you earn money over time. This makes it the best choice for long-term affiliate marketing.

### 2. Typeform Affiliate Program
![a screenshot of typeform's affiliate program](https://formester-strapi.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/a_screenshot_of_typeform_s_affiliate_program_8877d0ab1a.png)

**Earnings:** $20 upfront + 15 percent recurring until $500

Typeform offers a unique combination of instant and recurring rewards. You earn $20 immediately when someone upgrades to a paid plan and then 15 percent of their monthly subscription until you reach $500. This gives both quick payouts and ongoing income.

You don’t need to be a Typeform user to join. Affiliates receive banners, templates, and promotional materials. They can share these on blogs, social media, YouTube videos, landing pages, or in email campaigns.

**Who it’s for:** Creators who suggest interactive, conversation-style forms. They want a mix of quick and steady earnings.

**Why it ranks second:** Typeform is the only program that offers upfront bonuses and recurring commissions. This lets affiliates earn money right away and over time.

### 3. Fillout Affiliate Program
![a screenshot of fillout's affiliate program](https://formester-strapi.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/a_screenshot_of_fillout_s_affiliate_program_aa2e2bc4d8.png)

**Earnings:** 30 percent recurring commission for up to 12 months

Fillout pays a 30 percent recurring commission for anyone who signs up for a paid plan through your referral link. Earnings continue monthly for up to 12 months, giving a reliable short-term revenue stream.

Joining is free and simple. You get a personalized referral link to share via blogs, social media, email campaigns, or digital marketing efforts.

**Who it’s for:** Tech creators, SaaS marketers, and digital entrepreneurs who recommend simple, powerful form builders.

**Why it ranks third:** Fillout has a high commission rate. However, earnings stop after a year. This makes it less appealing for long-term passive income than Formester or Typeform.

### 4. Jotform Affiliate Program
![a screenshot of jotform's affiliate program](https://formester-strapi.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/a_screenshot_of_jotform_s_affiliate_program_4ad0b785d9.png)

**Earnings:** 30 percent commission per new paid user

Jotform lets you earn a commission for every new paid user who signs up through your referral link. There’s no complex setup or minimum requirement — just share your link and start earning.

You can promote Jotform through online marketing, social media, websites, or networks. The more referrals you generate, the higher your recurring income.

**Who it’s for:** Freelancers, agencies, and content creators recommending productivity or no code tools.

**Why it ranks fourth:** The commission rate is good, but the program does not offer lifetime payouts or upfront bonuses. This makes it less attractive for long-term affiliate marketing.

### 5. WPForms Partner Program
![a screenshot of wpforms affiliate program](https://formester-strapi.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/a_screenshot_of_wpforms_affiliate_program_644c720fed.png)

**Earnings:** 20 percent commission per sale

WPForms pays a 20 percent commission on every sale through your referral link. You can promote it via blogs, websites, YouTube videos, social media, or email campaigns.

WPForms is easy to use. It has templates, conditional logic, file uploads, and great support. This makes it a good choice for content creators, bloggers, and WordPress users.

**Who it’s for:** Bloggers, small businesses, and anyone recommending a reliable WordPress form plugin.

**Why it ranks fifth:** WPForms is reliable but has a lower commission rate. This makes it better for smaller, WordPress-focused audiences instead of full-time affiliates.

### Final Thoughts
**Joining form builder affiliate programs** is an easy way to earn recurring income. You promote tools your audience needs, save them time, and provide real value — all while generating revenue for yourself.

By using affiliate product links, coupon codes, and landing pages, you can increase conversion rates. This way, you earn a commission for every referral. You can earn money in many ways. You can write a blog post, make YouTube videos, or run email marketing campaigns.

For the highest recurring commission and long-term support, **Formester is a standout choice**. Typeform, Fillout, Jotform, and WPForms also provide strong earning opportunities depending on your audience and digital marketing strategy.

Pick the program that fits your style, start sharing, and turn your recommendations into a **steady income stream.**

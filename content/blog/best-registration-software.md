<h2>Which registration software should you choose?</h2>

<p>Registration forms ask for more than a contact form does. They usually need to take a payment, stop accepting entries when a session fills, send a confirmation the attendee can show at the door, and in healthcare they need HIPAA behind all of it.</p>

<p>Almost every tool below handles the form. What separates them is which of those four things they handle without an add-on, and what that costs.</p>

<div class="tldr">
<p><strong>The 60-second TL;DR</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Best all-around:</strong> Formester, unlimited registrations with payments on the free plan</li>
<li><strong>Best for HIPAA registration:</strong> Jotform, HIPAA on Gold at $129/mo</li>
<li><strong>Best for capacity and pricing logic:</strong> Cognito Forms, from $24/mo</li>
<li><strong>Best modern editor:</strong> Fillout, 1,000 free responses and unlimited seats</li>
<li><strong>Best for event landing pages:</strong> Paperform, from $29/mo</li>
<li><strong>Best cheapest paid tier:</strong> Zoho Forms, $10/mo billed annually</li>
<li><strong>Best free unlimited option:</strong> Tally</li>
<li><strong>Best for WordPress event sites:</strong> Gravity Forms, from $59/year</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2>Which registration software should you actually pick?</h2>

<p>Start with money. If you need to take payment on a free plan, only Formester and Cognito Forms do that. Everything else asks you to upgrade first, and Google Forms and Tally cannot take payment at all.</p>

<p>Then check whether capacity matters. If registration must close automatically when a session fills, Cognito Forms handles it natively and most others do not.</p>

<p>HIPAA narrows things sharply: Zoho Forms at $90 billed annually, Jotform at $129, Cognito Forms at $174, or Formstack with a BAA from $99. If none of those constraints apply, the decision is mostly about volume and how predictable it is.</p>

### Why Use Password Protection on Forms
Password protection adds a secure login page before your form or survey. This means that only respondents with the correct code can access the form. It’s an essential feature for situations such as:

- Private surveys for employees or research participants

- Exclusive content or premium offers for members or clients

- Sensitive data collection, including medical, legal, or financial records

- Client onboarding forms containing confidential information

- Internal audits or confidential feedback within organizations

By using a password, you control who can see and submit your forms. **This keeps your data safe, private, and professional.**


### Step-by-Step Guide to Setting Up Password Protection
Here’s how to create a password-protected form in Formester:


#### 1. Login and Create a Form
Start by logging in to Formester and creating a new form. To save time, you can use the AI form builder](/ai-form-generator/)** which automatically generates a professional structure.

#### 2. Customize Your Form
Adjust the form’s design to match your brand. You can also turn on features that make your forms better. These include conditional logic, notifications, and automated responses.

#### 3. Add a Login Page

- Hover over the Add Page button in the bottom left corner.

- Select Login Page. This page will appear before all other pages in your form.

#### 4. Set the Password

- Click on the login page element to open General Properties.

- Customize the labels and description as needed.

- Click Set Password and enter your code.

**Important security note:**

- The password is never stored in Formester’s database.

- Only the admin can create or update it.

- If you forget the password, you can always return, set a new one, and publish. Changes take effect immediately.

#### 5. Share and Publish
Once the password is set, publish your form and share it with your respondents. Only users with the **correct password** will be able to access and submit the form.

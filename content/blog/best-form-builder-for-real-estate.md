<h2>Which form builder should a real estate team choose?</h2>

<p>Real estate asks things of a form that most builders were not designed for. An open house sign-in has to work when the listing has no Wi-Fi. A rental application has to take a screening fee and accept three months of pay stubs as uploads.</p>

<p>Buyer intake has to branch, so a first-time buyer sees mortgage questions and an investor sees yield questions. Very few tools do all four, which is what the list below sorts on.</p>

<h2>Which real estate form builder should you actually pick?</h2>

<p>Start with the open house. If sign-ins happen where signal is unreliable, only Formester and Zoho Forms capture offline, and everything else loses the entry.</p>

<p>Then check the money. Application and screening fees on a free plan mean Formester or Cognito Forms; everywhere else you upgrade first, and Typeform, Tally and Google Forms cannot take payment at all.</p>

<p>After that it is about what your forms are for. Rental applications with heavy uploads point to Jotform, fee and commission maths to Cognito Forms, and buyer intake that needs finishing to Typeform.</p>

<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>

<details><summary>What is the best form builder for real estate?</summary><div>The best form builder for real estate is Formester for most agents, because it captures open house sign-ins offline and takes application fees on the free plan. Jotform is the better answer for rental applications with heavy document uploads.</div></details>

<details><summary>Which form builder works offline for open house sign-ins?</summary><div>Two tools here capture offline: Formester stores entries locally and syncs when the connection returns, and Zoho Forms offers the same through its mobile apps. <details><summary>Can I collect application fees on a real estate form?</summary><div>Yes, two tools include payment collection on their free plans. <details><summary>Which form builder handles rental applications best?</summary><div>Jotform handles rental applications best, because its templates cover tenant screening and its file uploads take pay stubs, bank statements and ID scans. Cognito Forms is the better pick when the application needs fee calculations or multiple applicants on one lease.</div></details>

<details><summary>Do real estate form builders connect to a CRM?</summary><div>Most connect through integrations rather than natively. HubSpot Forms and Zoho Forms feed their own CRMs directly, Formstack maps into Salesforce, and tools like Formester route into Chime, Follow Up Boss or kvCORE through Zapier.</div></details>

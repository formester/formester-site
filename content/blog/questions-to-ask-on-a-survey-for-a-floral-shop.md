## Why Use a Survey for a Floral Shop?
Running a floral shop is not just about selling flowers. It is about bringing joy to customers. You must meet their expectations and stay ahead of the competition.

A survey helps you connect with your customers and understand their preferences. It’s an easy way to gather feedback, improve your services, and even boost sales.

For example, you can ask customers what types of bouquets they like or if they’d be interested in a subscription service. With their answers, you’ll know exactly what they want, so you can offer products and services that make them happy.

### Benefits of Surveys for Floral Shops:
* <strong>Understand Customer Preferences:</strong> Learn their favorite flowers or arrangements.

* <strong>Improve Customer Experience:</strong> Identify areas where you can do better, like delivery or packaging.

* <strong>Improve Sales:</strong> Offer products or services based on customer needs.

* <strong>Stay Competitive:</strong> Keep up with trends and what your competitors might be doing.

<h2 class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__h2" id="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq-h2">Frequently asked questions</h2>
<p class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__intro">Common questions about running a customer feedback survey for a floral shop.</p>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__list">

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>What questions should I ask in a floral shop customer survey?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">Cover 5 categories: customer experience (greeting, store cleanliness), product quality (freshness, arrangement, packaging), pricing (perceived value), delivery (on-time rate, condition), and loyalty (repeat visits, NPS). Aim for 15-20 questions total.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>How long should a floral shop survey be?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">5 to 10 minutes. 15-20 questions. Past 10 minutes, completion rate drops below 50%. Use a mix of yes/no, linear scale, and 1 or 2 short-text questions.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>When is the best time to send a floral shop survey?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">Within 24 hours of purchase or delivery, while the experience is fresh. For wedding or event arrangements, send 3-5 days after the event so the customer has seen how the flowers held up.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>How many responses do I need for a floral shop survey to be useful?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">30 responses give you a rough read on trends. 100 responses give statistically meaningful comparisons by store, day, or product line.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>Should I offer an incentive for a floral shop survey?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">Yes if response rate is low. A 10% discount on the next purchase or a free single-stem bouquet works. Avoid cash incentives, they attract low-quality responses.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>What's a good NPS score for a floral shop?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">Above 50 is excellent. 30-50 is good. 0-30 is average. Below 0 means most customers are detractors and you have urgent service issues to fix.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>How do I survey wedding and event customers vs walk-in customers?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">Use two different surveys. Walk-in customers care about freshness, pricing, and store experience. Wedding customers care about responsiveness, design approval process, day-of delivery, and final product matching the vision.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>What linear-scale labels work best for floral surveys?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">1-5 with labels 'Very dissatisfied / Dissatisfied / Neutral / Satisfied / Very satisfied' works for product quality. NPS uses 0-10 with 'Not at all likely / Extremely likely' to recommend.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__item">
<summary>Can I send the floral shop survey by SMS?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">Yes. Send the survey link in a text after the purchase. Texts have higher open rates than email (98% vs 25%), but customers may resist long forms on mobile, keep mobile surveys under 8 questions.</div>
</details>

<summary>Is there a free template for a floral shop survey?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-tmpl-faq__answer">Yes. Formester's floral and small-business templates include pre-built surveys with the 20 questions above. Free to use, customize, and embed on your floral shop website or share via SMS.</div>
</details>

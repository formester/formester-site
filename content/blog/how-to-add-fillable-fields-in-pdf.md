## What Is a Fillable PDF?
A fillable PDF is an **editable PDF** with interactive elements like:

- Text fields

- Images

- Signature boxes

People can complete forms digitally, without printing. You can open a PDF online, fill it out, and save your file, all in just a few clicks.

**This is ideal for:**

- **[Job applications](/templates/categories/job-application-forms)**

- Registration forms

- Consent documents

- Feedback surveys

- Contracts or agreements

## How to Add Fillable Fields to a PDF in Formester
Here’s how to turn any document into a smart, fillable PDF form in minutes.

### 1. Create Your Form
![a screenshot of formester's form builder](https://formester-strapi.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/a_screenshot_of_formester_s_form_builder_9465ca1ae4.png)

Start by building your form in Formester.

- Log in to your dashboard

- Click Create New Form

- Add the questions or fields you want

- Drag and drop options like name, email, text box, dropdown, or signature

- Then connect this form to PDF editor under advanced tab

Ensure the form includes all the field types you want in the final PDF. This includes text fields, dates, and multiple choice options.

### 2. Upload a Blank PDF or Image File

![a screenshot of formester's pdf editor](https://formester-strapi.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/a_screenshot_of_formester_s_pdf_editor_f03dc6c4a7.png)

- Now, go to the PDF Settings tab.

- Upload a blank PDF, Word document, or even an image file like JPG or PNG

- This will act as your form layout, the final document that gets auto-filled

**Tip:** Make sure the PDF has space for each answer, or clear boxes where fields should go.

### 3. Add Fillable Form Fields to the PDF
![a screenshot of formester's pdf editor](https://formester-strapi.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/a_screenshot_of_formester_s_pdf_editor_c3c5335a1a.png)

Here’s where you connect your form to the PDF layout.

- Use the drag-and-drop PDF editor to place boxes on top of your document

- Choose the right field type: text field, checkbox, dropdown, signature, etc.

- Use the @ sign to map each field to your form

**Example:** @name, @email, @feedback, @signature

This ensures that when someone fills out your form, their answers go exactly where they should in the final PDF.

### 4. Save Your Document and Publish
![a screenshot of formester's publish ui](https://formester-strapi.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/a_screenshot_of_formester_s_publish_ui_cb3ce3fc71.png)
**Once all fields are mapped:**

- Click Save Your Document

- Publish the form

- Share the form link or embed it on your website

- Now your fillable PDF form is live and ready.

### 5. Complete Forms and Auto-Fill PDFs
![a screenshot of formester's results tab](https://formester-strapi.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/a_screenshot_of_formester_s_results_tab_28d65561c6.png)

**When someone submits your form:**

- Their answers instantly fill the correct spots in your PDF

- The completed form is ready to save your file, download, or send by email

- You can even sign documents online using the signature field

- All of this works smoothly across desktops and mobile devices

No more printing, scanning, or back-and-forth **emails**.

## What Makes a Good Form Submission Email?

**Confirm the action**
Your user must know the form worked. A clear line at the top builds trust.

**Tell them what comes next**
When you guide the next step, the user feels supported.

**Deliver value**
If you promised something, give it right away.

**Focus on one goal**
Do not overload the first email. Keep it simple.

**Match your brand**
The email should feel like a natural part of your site.

### 10 Best Form Submission Email Templates To Try
Each template includes a sample AI prompt you can use inside Formester.

### Simple Confirmation Email

**Subject**: We received your submission

~~~
Hi [First Name],

Thanks for reaching out. We have received your form.

Your message helps us understand what you need. Our team will go through your submission and get back to you if we need more details.

We appreciate your time.

Best,
[Your Name]
[Your Position]
[Your Company]
~~~

>**AI Prompt Example**
Write a simple confirmation email. Keep the tone friendly and clear. Tell the user we received their form and will reply soon.

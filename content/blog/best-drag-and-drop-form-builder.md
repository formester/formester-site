<h2>Which drag and drop form builder should you choose?</h2>

<p>Every tool here lets you drag a field onto a canvas, so the builder itself is rarely what separates them. What separates them is what happens once the form is live.</p>

<p>Two things decide most of it: whether the tool counts your responses, and how much of the editor's power sits behind a paid tier. The nine below are ordered on that.</p>

<div class="tldr">
<p><strong>The 60-second TL;DR</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Best all-around:</strong> Formester, unlimited responses on the free plan</li>
<li><strong>Best for feature depth:</strong> Jotform, from $39/mo</li>
<li><strong>Best for Airtable and Notion teams:</strong> Fillout, from $19/mo</li>
<li><strong>Best for WordPress:</strong> Gravity Forms, from $59/year</li>
<li><strong>Best for conversational surveys:</strong> Typeform, from $39/mo</li>
<li><strong>Best free option:</strong> Google Forms</li>
<li><strong>Best inside Microsoft 365:</strong> Microsoft Forms</li>
<li><strong>Best cheapest paid tier:</strong> Zoho Forms, $10/mo billed annually</li>
</ul>
</div>

<p><img src="https://formester-strapi.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/formester_99e2a99609.png" alt="Formester homepage hero screenshot" style="width:100%;max-width:900px;height:auto;border-radius:8px;border:1px solid #e5e7eb;margin:16px 0;" loading="lazy" /></p>

<p>Formester never meters you. Unlimited forms, unlimited responses and every field type come with the free plan, so a form that suddenly performs does not change what you pay.</p>

<p>The editor is quick. Interactions land immediately, the live preview tracks every change, and forms come out mobile-responsive without extra work.</p>

<p>Field settings sit in a right-hand panel that stays out of the way until you need it. AI generation takes a prompt, a PDF or a pasted question list, and it runs on the free plan.</p>

<p>The payoff for pushing through is depth. Whatever you are building, someone has already built a template for it.</p>

<p>Fillout treats Airtable and Notion as the source of truth rather than integrations to tick off. Forms write back into your tables, editing existing records and matching on a key.</p>

<p>The block editor moves quickly: drag a field in, click it, edit it, move on. Multi-step forms with a progress bar are the default rather than a setting you hunt for.</p>

<p>Its free plan allows 1,000 responses a month with unlimited seats, which is unusually generous for a tool at this price.</p>

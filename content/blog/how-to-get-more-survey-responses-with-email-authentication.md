## Why Email Deliverability Matters for Surveys
If your survey emails are not delivered, you lose responses and waste time. Poor deliverability leads to fewer insights and weaker data.

High deliverability means your emails consistently reach the inbox, giving you a better chance of getting clicks and completed surveys. That’s why improving it should be your top priority.

### Common Reasons Survey Invitations Land in Spam
Here’s why many survey invitations fail to reach the inbox:

- Weak or poor sender reputation.

- Missing SPF, DKIM, or DMARC records.

- Spam-triggering subject lines.

- Too many images or too little text.

- Sending from a free domain instead of your own.

- Using an outdated email list full of inactive addresses.

### Understanding Email Authentication
Think of email authentication like an ID check. It proves the email is really from you.

**The three main protocols are:**

- SPF (Sender Policy Framework): Tells mail servers which IP addresses can send emails for your domain.

- DKIM (DomainKeys Identified Mail): Adds a secure digital signature to verify your emails.

- DMARC (Domain-based Message Authentication, Reporting, and Conformance): Decides what to do if your email fails SPF or DKIM checks.

When all three are set up correctly, you greatly increase your chances of landing in the inbox.

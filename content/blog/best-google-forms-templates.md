<h3 class="fmstr-cmp-bgft-div__h3"><span>1.</span> Event registration form</h3>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bgft-div__body">
<p>Use this when you have an in-person or virtual event and need name, email, session choice, and dietary preference. The Google Forms version covers the basics. For multi-session conferences with track-based capacity limits, use the <a href="https://formester.com/templates/categories/event-management/">Formester event registration template</a>, which ships per-session caps and confirmation emails.</p><span class="fmstr-cmp-bgft-div__upgrade-label">Upgrade with Formester</span><p>Open the <a href="https://formester.com/templates/categories/event-management/">Formester event registration template</a> when you need per-session capacity caps, branded confirmation emails, conditional questions, and a single response file across all tracks. None of these are configurable in Google Forms without an Apps Script workaround.</p>

<h3 class="fmstr-cmp-bgft-div__h3"><span>4.</span> Customer feedback form</h3>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bgft-div__body">
<p>A 5-question feedback form gets a 3-4x higher completion rate than a 15-question one (<a href="https://www.surveymonkey.com/curiosity/survey_completion_times/">SurveyMonkey research on survey length</a>). This template ships five: overall satisfaction, what you liked most, what could improve, likelihood to recommend (NPS 0-10), and a free-text open question. Don't expand past 8 unless you're paying respondents.</p><p>The NPS question uses Google Forms' &ldquo;linear scale&rdquo; set to 0-10. Label the ends &ldquo;Not at all likely&rdquo; and &ldquo;Extremely likely&rdquo; exactly. The scoring math (promoter = 9-10, passive = 7-8, detractor = 0-6) you'll calculate in the linked Google Sheet. For automatic NPS scoring and segmentation by customer cohort, use the <a href="https://formester.com/templates/categories/customer-surveys/">Formester NPS template</a>.</p><span class="fmstr-cmp-bgft-div__upgrade-label">Upgrade with Formester</span><p>The <a href="https://formester.com/templates/categories/feedback-forms/">Formester customer feedback template</a> ships automatic NPS calculation, brand-controlled colors, conditional follow-ups (rating = 9-10, &ldquo;Would you leave a review?&rdquo; appears; rating = 0-6, &ldquo;What went wrong?&rdquo; appears), and a shareable dashboard you can send to leadership instead of a raw spreadsheet.</p>

<h3 class="fmstr-cmp-bgft-div__h3"><span>5.</span> Survey and quiz template</h3>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bgft-div__body">
<p>This is two different jobs glued into one template. Surveys collect opinions (no right answer). Quizzes test knowledge (graded). Google Forms supports both via the &ldquo;Make this a quiz&rdquo; toggle in Settings, but the UX is shared, which is why both end up in the same template.</p><p>For a teacher running a weekly comprehension check, the quiz mode is fine: set the toggle, add point values per question, set correct answers, share. <h2 class="fmstr-cmp-bgft-div__h2" id="how-to-use-template" style="margin-top: 0;">How to use a Google Forms template in 3 steps</h2>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bgft-div__body">
<p>The walkthrough below applies to every template in this guide and every template in <a href="https://docs.google.com/forms/u/0/?ftv=1" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Google Forms' native gallery</a>.</p>
</div>
<ol class="fmstr-cmp-bgft-div__steps">
<li class="fmstr-cmp-bgft-div__step"><h4>Open the template</h4><p>Click &ldquo;Make a copy&rdquo; on the Google Forms link. The form lands in your Google Drive as a new file. If you're not signed in to Google, you'll be prompted before the copy completes.</p></li>
<li class="fmstr-cmp-bgft-div__step"><h4>Customize the title, questions, and settings</h4><p>Change the title and description at the top. Edit each question by clicking it; the side panel lets you change question type (short answer, multiple choice, dropdown, linear scale, file upload, etc.). Mark questions required by toggling the asterisk. For settings (collect email addresses, allow editing after submit, confirmation message, response receipts), click the gear icon in the top-right.</p></li>
<li class="fmstr-cmp-bgft-div__step"><h4>Share</h4><p>Click &ldquo;Send&rdquo; in the top-right. Three options: email (Google sends), link (copy-paste anywhere), or embed (iframe HTML for your site). For tracking response source across channels, add UTM parameters to the link or use <a href="https://formester.com/features/form-analytics/">Formester's native source tracking</a>.</p></li>
</ol>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bgft-div__body" style="margin-top: 18px;">
<p>If you find yourself doing more than 10 minutes of customization, it's faster to start in a tool built for it. <a href="https://formester.com/features/drag-and-drop-form-builder/">Formester's drag-and-drop builder</a> takes the same templates further: conditional logic, branding, payments, and a real response dashboard.</p>

<div class="fmstr-cmp-bgft-faq__container">
<h2 class="fmstr-cmp-bgft-faq__h2" id="fmstr-cmp-bgft-faq-h2">Google Forms templates FAQ</h2>
<p class="fmstr-cmp-bgft-faq__intro">Answers that mirror the FAQPage JSON-LD on the live page.</p>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bgft-faq__list">

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bgft-faq__item">
<summary>Where do I find Google Forms templates?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bgft-faq__answer">Two places. First, the native template gallery at <a href="https://docs.google.com/forms/u/0/?ftv=1" rel="noopener" target="_blank">forms.google.com</a>, which ships seven templates including contact info, event registration, party invite, t-shirt sign-up, find-a-time, RSVP, exit ticket, course evaluation, assessment, worksheet, and blank quiz. Second, third-party galleries: the 26 templates in this guide, <a href="https://formester.com/templates/">Formester's 800+ template library</a>, and other community galleries on the web.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bgft-faq__item">
<summary>How do I copy a Google Forms template?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bgft-faq__answer">Click "Make a copy" on the Google Forms link. The form lands in your Google Drive as a new file you fully own. If you're not signed in to Google, you'll be prompted before the copy completes. Once copied, every question, setting, and section is editable.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bgft-faq__item">
<summary>Are Google Forms templates really free?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bgft-faq__answer">Yes. Both Google's native gallery and the templates in this guide are free to copy and edit. There is no Google Workspace requirement for personal accounts. The Formester equivalents are also free to open and use on Formester's free tier (up to 3 forms, 200 monthly responses); paid plans unlock conditional logic, branding, and higher limits.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bgft-faq__item">
<summary>Can I customize a Google Forms template after I copy it?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bgft-faq__answer">Yes. Edit each question by clicking it; the side panel lets you change question type (short answer, multiple choice, dropdown, linear scale, file upload, date, time, multiple-choice grid, checkbox grid). Mark any question required by toggling the asterisk. For form-level settings (collect email addresses, confirmation message, response receipts), click the gear icon in the top-right.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bgft-faq__item">
<summary>How do I make a Google Forms template look on-brand?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bgft-faq__answer">Limited options inside Google Forms: change the theme color, add a header image, change the font (Decorative, Formal, Casual, Basic). For a logo, custom colors that match your brand exactly, or a custom URL, use <a href="https://formester.com/features/branding-kit/">Formester's branding kit</a>, which ships full white-labeling on paid plans.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bgft-faq__item">
<summary>Can a Google Forms template collect payments?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bgft-faq__answer">Not natively. Google Forms does not integrate with Stripe, PayPal, or any payment processor. The workaround is linking to a separate payment page after submission, which loses 20-40% of buyers at the handoff. For forms that need to take payment inline (order forms, donations, event tickets, paid registrations), use a <a href="https://formester.com/features/online-payments/">Formester template with native payments</a>.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bgft-faq__item">
<summary>Can I add conditional logic to a Google Forms template?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bgft-faq__answer">Partially. Google Forms supports section-based branching: based on a multiple-choice answer, send the respondent to a different section. It does not support per-question conditional logic (show/hide individual fields based on previous answers). For per-question branching, see the <a href="https://formester.com/blog/how-to-add-conditional-questions-in-google-forms/">conditional questions in Google Forms guide</a> or use <a href="https://formester.com/features/conditional-logic/">Formester's visual rule builder</a>.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-bgft-faq__item">
<summary>Where do submitted Google Forms responses go?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-bgft-faq__answer">By default, into the form itself (the Responses tab). Click the green Sheets icon to link a Google Sheet for live response export. For a full walkthrough of viewing, filtering, and managing responses, see <a href="https://formester.com/blog/5-ways-to-view-responses-in-google-forms/">5 ways to view responses in Google Forms</a>.</div>
</details>

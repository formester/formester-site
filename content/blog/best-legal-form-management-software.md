<h2>Which legal form management software should you choose?</h2>

<p>Law firms buying form software are usually solving one of two problems. Either intake is messy and clients arrive through email and phone notes, or document production is slow because the same details get retyped into every engagement letter.</p>

<p>Those problems have different answers. Legal practice platforms handle the first and cost per user. Form and document tools handle the second for a flat fee. Buying the wrong category is how firms end up paying for software they use once a month.</p>

<div class="tldr">
<p><strong>The 60-second TL;DR</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Best flat-rate intake forms:</strong> Formester, unlimited responses free</li>
<li><strong>Best for HIPAA-adjacent intake:</strong> Jotform Gold, $129/mo</li>
<li><strong>Best for document generation:</strong> Formstack Documents, from $299/mo</li>
<li><strong>Best legal intake CRM:</strong> Clio Grow, from $49/user/mo</li>

</ul>

<h2>How to pick the right legal form management software</h2>

<p>Decide first whether you are buying software for the whole firm or for one job. Clio Grow, Lawmatics and MyCase are firm-wide systems priced per user, and they only pay back if they replace tools you currently pay for separately.</p>

<p>If intake is the specific problem, a flat-rate form tool costs a fraction of that. Formester covers straightforward intake, Cognito Forms handles multi-party matters, and Jotform Gold is the affordable route when HIPAA is required.</p>

<p>If the real cost is document production rather than intake, Formstack Documents addresses that directly, and no amount of better forms will fix it.</p>

<h2>Common questions about legal form management software</h2>

<details><summary>What is legal form management software?</summary><div>Legal form management software collects client information and turns it into usable records for a law firm. Some tools focus on the intake form itself, while others extend into matter creation, document generation and billing across the whole practice.</div></details>

<details><summary>What is the best free legal intake form tool?</summary><div>The best free option for legal intake is Formester, whose free plan includes unlimited forms and responses with e-signature and payment collection. Cognito Forms offers conditional logic free with a 100-entry monthly limit. Neither offers HIPAA.</div></details>

<details><summary>Which legal form tools offer HIPAA compliance?</summary><div>For HIPAA, Jotform offers HIPAA features from its Gold plan at $129 a month, Formstack supports HIPAA with a BAA, Cognito Forms offers it on Enterprise at $174, and Typeform on quoted Enterprise plans. Formester does not offer HIPAA.</div></details>

<details><summary>How much does legal intake software cost?</summary><div>Legal intake software ranges from free to $150 per user a month. Flat-rate form tools start at $13 for Formester and $24 for Cognito Forms, while per-user practice platforms start at $25 for LawDroid Copilot, $49 for Clio, and $60 for MyCase.</div></details>

<details><summary>Should a small firm use a practice platform or a form builder?</summary><div>A small firm should use a form builder if intake is the only problem and a practice platform if it is replacing several tools. At $60 per user a month, a ten-person firm pays $600 for MyCase, against $49 a month for a flat-rate form tool covering 25 people.</div></details>

<details><summary>Can these tools generate engagement letters automatically?</summary><div>Yes, Formstack Documents populates Word and PDF templates from intake answers, and Cognito Forms generates documents from entries. Practice platforms like Clio and MyCase include document assembly as part of the wider system.</div></details>

<details><summary>Which tool works best with Clio?</summary><div>Clio Grow works best with Clio because it is part of the same platform, so an intake converts into a matter without rekeying. Lawmatics and several form tools also integrate with Clio for firms that prefer a separate intake layer.</div></details>

<details><summary>Do any of these use AI for intake?</summary><div>Yes, LawDroid offers a conversational AI intake chatbot from $99 a month and an AI assistant, Copilot, at $25 per user. Formester and Jotform both include AI form generation, though that builds the form rather than conducting the intake.</div></details>

<h2>Which lead capture software should you choose?</h2>

<p>Lead capture is two jobs wearing one name. The first is collecting the details, which every tool here does. The second is qualifying and routing what you collected, which is where they diverge sharply.</p>

<p>If the form is the whole job, the cheapest capable tool wins. If the lead has to be scored, segmented and pushed into a CRM, the form matters far less than what sits behind it.</p>

<h2>Which lead capture tool should you actually pick?</h2>

<p>Decide first whether the lead needs to land somewhere automatically. If it does, HubSpot and Zoho Forms do it natively at opposite ends of the price range, and Formstack does it for Salesforce.</p>

<p>If the form itself is the job, volume decides. Formester and Tally place no cap on leads, HubSpot allows unlimited submissions free, and everything else meters you at some point.</p>

<p>Then check what you need the form to work out. Cognito Forms scores a lead inside the form, Typeform gets more of the questions answered, and Fillout keeps the record in the database you already use.</p>

<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>

<details><summary>What is the best lead capture software?</summary><div>The best lead capture software for most teams is Formester, because it places no cap on leads and includes AI generation and drop-off analytics on the free plan. HubSpot Forms is the better answer when the CRM matters more than the form itself.</div></details>

<details><summary>Can I capture unlimited leads for free?</summary><div>Yes, three tools here allow it. Formester places no cap on forms or responses on any plan, Tally offers unlimited forms and submissions free, and HubSpot Forms allows unlimited submissions with its branding on the form.</div></details>

<details><summary>Which lead capture tool connects to a CRM?</summary><div>HubSpot Forms and Zoho Forms both connect natively to their own CRMs at no extra step, and Formstack maps fields into Salesforce records directly. Most other tools connect through integrations or Zapier rather than natively.</div></details>

<details><summary>How much does lead capture software cost?</summary><div>Lead capture tools here start at $10 a month for Zoho Forms billed annually and $13 for Formester, rising to $99 for Formstack. Free plans from Formester, Tally and HubSpot handle unlimited leads at no cost.</div></details>

<details><summary>Do multi-step forms capture more leads?</summary><div>Multi-step forms generally capture more completions on longer qualification flows, because asking for the email first secures a partial lead before the harder questions. Fillout and Typeform both build this way by default.</div></details>

<details><summary>Which tool is best for qualifying leads?</summary><div>Cognito Forms is the strongest for qualification, because calculated fields can score a lead inside the form based on budget, timeline and company size. Typeform gets more of those questions answered by asking them one at a time.</div></details>

<details><summary>Can I collect payments on a lead capture form?</summary><div>Yes, several tools support it. <details><summary>What is the catch with free lead capture tools?</summary><div>Free plans usually take one of three things: they cap responses, they put vendor branding on the form, or they hold back the CRM connection. Checking which applies matters more than comparing the free feature lists.</div></details>

<h2>Verdict</h2>

<p>Most teams overbuy on the form and underbuy on what happens next. The form is rarely why a lead goes cold; the routing usually is.</p>

<p>If leads should land in a CRM, HubSpot does it free and Zoho does it for $10 a month. If the form is the whole job and volume is the worry, Formester caps nothing at any tier. If each lead is worth real money, Typeform's completion advantage pays for its price.</p>

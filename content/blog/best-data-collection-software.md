<h2>Which data collection software should you choose?</h2>

<p>Data collection covers a wide range of work. A field team logging observations offline has almost nothing in common with a marketing team running a customer survey, yet both search for the same phrase.</p>

<p>What separates the nine tools below is where they stop. Some cap responses. Some need connectivity. Some only make sense if you already pay for the surrounding suite. Those limits, rather than the editors, decide which one fits.</p>

<div class="tldr">
<p><strong>The 60-second TL;DR</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Best all-around:</strong> Formester, unlimited responses free plus offline capture</li>
<li><strong>Best for feature depth:</strong> Jotform, 20,000+ templates, from $39/mo</li>
<li><strong>Best for completion rates:</strong> Typeform, one question at a time, from $39/mo</li>
<li><strong>Best free option:</strong> Google Forms, free with any Google account</li>
<li><strong>Best for Microsoft 365 tenants:</strong> Microsoft Forms, bundled with the suite</li>
<li><strong>Best for Airtable and Notion teams:</strong> Fillout, from $19/mo</li>
<li><strong>Best for Zoho users:</strong> Zoho Forms, from $10/mo billed annually</li>
<li><strong>Best for humanitarian field work:</strong> KoboToolbox, free for many organisations</li>
<li><strong>Best for enterprise research:</strong> SurveyMonkey, from $30/user/mo</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2>How we picked and ranked these nine tools</h2>

<p>Everything here comes from the vendors' own pricing pages and plan limits, read in full. In data collection the cap matters more than the feature list, because that is what stops a project mid-flight.</p>

<p>The weight went to what each free plan allows and what a full team costs, plus whether the tool works where the data actually gets collected. Several vendors advertise annual rates, so the monthly figures here are often higher than the ones on their pricing pages.</p>

<h2>1. Formester, best all-around</h2>

<p><img src="https://formester-strapi.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/formester_99e2a99609.png" alt="Formester homepage hero screenshot" style="width:100%;max-width:900px;height:auto;border-radius:8px;border:1px solid #e5e7eb;margin:16px 0;" loading="lazy" /></p>

<p>Formester never counts responses. Unlimited forms and unlimited responses come with the free plan, so a project that suddenly scales does not also suddenly cost money.</p>

<p>Offline capture is the other reason it leads this list. Forms install as an app on phones, tablets and laptops, hold entries on the device, then sync when a connection returns. Most tools below simply stop working without signal.</p>

<p>Paid tiers add capability rather than volume. Personal at $13 a month adds conditional logic, and Business at $49 covers a custom domain with 25 seats.</p>

<p><img src="https://formester-strapi.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/typeform_7aad7a2e5b.png" alt="Typeform homepage hero screenshot" style="width:100%;max-width:900px;height:auto;border-radius:8px;border:1px solid #e5e7eb;margin:16px 0;" loading="lazy" /></p>

<p>It is also the most expensive way to collect volume here. Free covers 10 responses a month, Basic is $39 with 100 responses, Plus $79 with 1,000, and Business $129 with 10,000.</p>

<p>There is no Forms-specific paid plan. It arrives with Google Workspace, where Business Starter is $7 per user a month on an annual basis, Standard is $14, and Plus is $22.</p>

<h2>5. Microsoft Forms, best for Microsoft 365 tenants</h2>

<p>Its published pricing now covers team plans only: Team Advantage at $30 per user a month and Team Premier at $92, with a three-seat minimum and $0.15 per response over your limit. Individual plans are no longer listed on the pricing page.</p>

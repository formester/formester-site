<h2>Which Cognito Forms alternative should you choose?</h2>

<p>People leave Cognito Forms for one of two reasons. Either the entry cap starts biting, since the free plan stops at 100 a month and even Pro allows only 2,000, or they want AI generation and a modern editor that Cognito Forms does not offer.</p>

<p>What they rarely want to give up is the calculation engine, which is genuinely the best in this category. Knowing which of those matters to you settles most of the list below.</p>

<div class="tldr">
<p><strong>The 60-second TL;DR</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Best overall alternative:</strong> Formester, unlimited responses with AI generation on the free plan</li>
<li><strong>Best when HIPAA is required:</strong> Jotform, HIPAA on Gold at $129/mo</li>
<li><strong>Best for Airtable or Notion teams:</strong> Fillout, writes into records directly</li>
<li><strong>Best for regulated industries:</strong> Formstack, from $99/mo with a BAA</li>
<li><strong>Best for AI-first building:</strong> forms.app</li>
<li><strong>Best for Zoho shops:</strong> Zoho Forms, from $10/mo billed annually</li>
<li><strong>Best plain replacement:</strong> Wufoo, from $22/mo</li>
<li><strong>Best free option:</strong> Google Forms</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2>1. Formester, the strongest overall Cognito Forms alternative</h2>

<p><img src="https://formester-strapi.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/formester_13bcec91de.png" alt="Formester homepage hero screenshot" style="width:100%;max-width:900px;height:auto;border-radius:8px;border:1px solid #e5e7eb;margin:16px 0;" loading="lazy" /></p>

<p>Formester removes the constraint that pushes most people off Cognito Forms. There is no entry cap, no form cap and no field cap on any plan, including the free one.</p>

<p>Payments work on the free plan through Stripe and PayPal, matching Cognito Forms there. Conditional logic sits on Personal at $13 a month, which is the one place Cognito Forms is more generous.</p>

<h2>2. Jotform, the alternative when HIPAA is non-negotiable</h2>

<p>Jotform is the obvious move if you need HIPAA without the price Cognito Forms attaches to it. Both put HIPAA on their top tier, but Jotform's Gold sits at $129 a month against Cognito Forms Enterprise at $174.</p>

<p>The trade is metering. Jotform counts submissions on every tier, so you swap one cap for another: 100 a month free, 1,000 on Bronze at $39.</p>

<p>Paid plans start at $19 a month, below Cognito Forms Pro at $24, with a considerably higher allowance.</p>

<h2>7. Wufoo, the alternative for straightforward no-frills forms</h2>

<p><img src="https://formester-strapi.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/wufoo_53925f167a.png" alt="Wufoo homepage hero screenshot" style="width:100%;max-width:900px;height:auto;border-radius:8px;border:1px solid #e5e7eb;margin:16px 0;" loading="lazy" /></p>

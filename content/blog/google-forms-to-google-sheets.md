<h2 class="fmstr-cmp-gfgs-faq__h2" id="fmstr-cmp-gfgs-faq-h2">Google Forms to Google Sheets FAQ</h2>
<p class="fmstr-cmp-gfgs-faq__intro">Answers that mirror the FAQPage JSON-LD on the live page.</p>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-gfgs-faq__list">

<details class="fmstr-cmp-gfgs-faq__item">
<summary>Is there a way to link a Google Form to Google Sheets?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-gfgs-faq__answer">Yes. Open the Responses tab, click the green Google Sheets icon, pick &ldquo;Create a new spreadsheet&rdquo; or &ldquo;Select existing spreadsheet.&rdquo; Every new submission lands as a row in the Sheet automatically. No script, no add-on.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-gfgs-faq__item">
<summary>Can a Google Form auto-populate a Google Sheet?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-gfgs-faq__answer">Yes. Once linked via the green Sheets icon, the Sheet updates in real time on every submission. New form questions added later show up as new columns the next time a response is submitted.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-gfgs-faq__item">
<summary>How to use Google Forms and Sheets together?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-gfgs-faq__answer">Three common patterns. First, the native auto-sync (covered above) for collecting responses. Second, Apps Script when you need conditional routing or custom logic. Third, Formester&rsquo;s Google Sheets integration when you also want branded forms, analytics, and conditional logic without writing code.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-gfgs-faq__item">
<summary>How do I open Google Sheets from Google Forms?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-gfgs-faq__answer">Inside Google Forms, open the Responses tab. The green Sheets icon top-right opens the linked Sheet in a new tab. Before linking, the icon shows the &ldquo;Create or select a spreadsheet&rdquo; modal.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-gfgs-faq__item">
<summary>How do I link an existing Google Sheet to a Google Form?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-gfgs-faq__answer">Open the form&rsquo;s Responses tab, click the green Sheets icon, pick &ldquo;Select existing spreadsheet&rdquo; instead of &ldquo;Create a new spreadsheet.&rdquo; Pick the Sheet from your Drive. Google adds a new tab called &ldquo;Form Responses 1&rdquo; inside it; your existing tabs and formulas are not touched.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-gfgs-faq__item">
<summary>Can a Google Form update an existing row in a Google Sheet?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-gfgs-faq__answer">Not natively. Each submission adds a new row. To update an existing row, you need Apps Script (match on a key column, find the row, write the new values) or a third-party tool. Formester&rsquo;s webhooks can post submission data to any endpoint that handles the update logic.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-gfgs-faq__item">
<summary>How do I unlink a Google Form from a Google Sheet?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-gfgs-faq__answer">In the form, open the Responses tab, click the three-dot menu next to the green Sheets icon, pick &ldquo;Unlink form.&rdquo; Past responses stay in the Sheet. Future responses are stored inside Google Forms only until you link a new Sheet.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-gfgs-faq__item">
<summary>How do I send responses from multiple forms into the same Google Sheet?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-gfgs-faq__answer">The native green-Sheets-icon flow only lets one Form write to one Sheet. To merge, either (a) link each form to its own Sheet then use <code>IMPORTRANGE()</code> or <code>QUERY()</code> to consolidate into a master Sheet, or (b) use Formester or Zapier which can route multiple forms to a single tab.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-gfgs-faq__item">
<summary>Why are my Google Sheet formulas being erased after a form submission?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-gfgs-faq__answer">Form submissions insert new rows at the top of the response tab and push old rows down; in-row formulas often get displaced or wiped. Move formulas to a separate analysis tab and reference the response tab with <code>QUERY()</code> or <code>IMPORTRANGE()</code>. Detailed thread on Stack Exchange.</div>
</details>

<details class="fmstr-cmp-gfgs-faq__item">
<summary>Is there a faster way than the green Sheets icon?</summary>
<div class="fmstr-cmp-gfgs-faq__answer">For one form, no. For repeat forms with branding, conditional logic, and analytics needs, Formester ships the same Sheet auto-sync plus a built-in dashboard, so you do not rebuild analysis in Sheets every time.</div>
</details>

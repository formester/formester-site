## What is an AI Form Builder?
An **[AI form generator](/ai-form-generator/)** is a tool that uses artificial intelligence to help you create online forms automatically. Instead of building from scratch, you can just describe your form or paste some content, and the AI will generate the right questions and structure for you.

**These tools often come with:**

- Smart templates

- Auto-generated questions

- Conditional logic

- Design customization

- Automation options


The goal is to help you build high-converting, user-friendly forms in minutes, even if you have no design or coding skills.

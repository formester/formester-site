**So you’ve hosted a successful event, but how do you really know if it hit the mark?**  You may have felt the energy in the room. You might have seen smiles and heard applause.

However, experiences can differ, and assumptions can be wrong. This is where post-event surveys come in.

**[Post-event surveys](/templates/categories/survey-forms)** gather honest feedback from your attendees. This feedback gives you useful insights. These insights help you plan future events, improve engagement, and **attract sponsors**.

In this guide, we will show you the **best post event survey questions to ask your attendees**. We will also give tips for writing these questions and explain how to use the results well.


## Why Post-Event Surveys Matter
Collecting feedback after an event is more than just a formality. Surveys help you:

- **Gauge Attendee Satisfaction:** Understand what your event attendees loved or didn’t love about your event.

- **Identify Strengths and Weaknesses:** Pinpoint specific aspects of the event that worked well and those that need improvement.

- **Measure Engagement and Loyalty:** Learn who is likely to attend this event again or recommend it to others.

- **Attract Sponsors and Support:** Concrete feedback and engagement metrics make it easier to secure sponsorships or funding.

- **Eliminate Guesswork:** Instead of assuming which session flopped, you have actual data from participants.

By gathering this feedback systematically, you can improve future events for attendees, speakers, and your organization.

### When and How to Send Post-Event Surveys
Timing and delivery matter. Here’s how to get the most responses:

- **Send Surveys Early:** Ideally within 1–2 business days of the event. Attendee impressions are freshest then.

- **Use Convenient Channels:** Email, **[QR codes](/features/qr-code-generator/)**, or mobile-friendly links work best. You can also embed surveys on your event website.

- **Keep it Short:** 5–10 questions are ideal to encourage completion.

- **Encourage participation by informing attendees that you value their feedback and will use it to improve future events. A small thank-you message goes a long way.

- **Reminders: Send one or two gentle reminders over the next two weeks, but avoid overdoing it.

### Types of Post-Event Survey Questions
Using the right question types ensures actionable insights. Here are the most effective options:

#### 1. Likert Scale Questions
A **[Likert scale](/blog/likert-scale/)** question helps you measure satisfaction or agreement on a scale. For example:

- How satisfied were you with the registration process?

- Strongly satisfied | Satisfied | Neutral | Dissatisfied | Strongly dissatisfied

#### 2. Open-Ended Questions
Encourage attendees to share detailed feedback:

- What was your favorite part of the event?

- What could we do to improve future events?

#### 3. Multiple Choice Questions
Provide predetermined answers for easy analysis:

**Which part of the event did you find most useful?**

- Keynote speaker

- Workshop A

- Workshop B

#### 4. Rating Scales
Quantify overall satisfaction using numbers or stars:

- On a scale of 1–10, how likely are you to recommend this virtual event or live session to a friend?

#### 5. Matrix Questions
Evaluate multiple items with the same set of options:

**Please indicate how strongly you agree with the following statements:**

- The sessions were well-organized

- The keynote speaker was engaging

- Networking opportunities were valuable

#### 6. Demographic Questions
Understand your audience for future planning:


- What is your role or job title?

- Which industry do you work in?

#### 7. File Upload Questions
Collect supporting materials like photos or documents:

- **[Upload a photo](/blog/how-to-upload-photo-to-online-form/)** from the event for your name tag or social media share.

#### 8. Slider Questions
Track intensity or frequency of engagement:

- How many sessions did you attend? (Slide 0–10)

#### 9. Benchmark Questions
Compare results to past events or industry standards:

- How does this event compare to other types of events you have attended?

### Tips for Writing Effective Survey Questions

- **Be Direct and Clear:** Avoid long or confusing questions.

- **Mix Question Types:** Include open-ended questions and multiple choice questions for actionable insights.

- **Avoid Bias:** Let attendees provide honest feedback.

- **Keep it Short:** Focus on the most important questions.

- **Use Scales Consistently:** This makes answers easier to analyze.

### Bonus: Pre-Event Survey Questions

Pre-event survey questions help you understand attendee expectations:

- How did you hear about this event?

- Which sessions are you most interested in?

- Do you have any dietary restrictions or accessibility needs?

These insights make your event more engaging and accessible from the start.

Luckily, Airtable lets you turn your tables into forms so you can collect form submissions directly into your database.

In this guide, you will learn how to create a form in Airtable. You will also learn how to manage form responses. Finally, you will discover how to make better and more flexible forms using **[Formester](/)**.

## Create a Form in Airtable
You can create a form in Airtable to collect data from others without requiring them to log in. Here is how to do it:

- Open the **Airtable base** where you want to collect information.

- Click the Dropdown next to the Views tab at the top left.

- Select Form View to start building your form.

- Airtable automatically pulls in fields from the form, based on your table’s structure.

- You can choose which fields to show or hide and rearrange the order by drag and drop.

- If you want your form to look more polished, you can add a cover image or a form description at the top.

- You can even set up single select options, linked record fields, and other common field types to guide your respondents.

- Once ready, share your form by clicking on the share form button. Copy the link and send it to others.

When you submit the form, all form submissions appear directly as new records in your Airtable base. You can review each form response, check the details like email address, and use record fields to organize the information.

### The Limitations of Airtable Forms
Airtable forms are helpful for basic data collection, but they do have some limits:

- Design options are minimal beyond adding a cover image.

- You cannot apply advanced logic or multi-page steps.

- Forms cannot easily create new records in connected apps without extra tools.

- You need paid plans for more advanced sharing or branding options.

- Limited automation options after a form is submitted.

- Customising field types beyond basic choices can be tricky.

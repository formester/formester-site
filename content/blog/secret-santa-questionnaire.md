A Secret Santa questionnaire is a short form each participant fills out so their gifter has enough specific information to skip the guesswork. Most go unused. 

The British Heart Foundation found that [32% of UK Secret Santa participant](https://www.bhf.org.uk/what-we-do/news-from-the-bhf/news-archive/2024/december/one-in-three-brits-admit-to-never-using-their-secret-santa-gifts)s never use the gift they received, and a 2021 Mastercard [survey](https://www.mastercard.com/news/europe/en-uk/newsroom/press-releases/en-gb/2021/november/what-do-brits-really-think-of-secret-santa/) found 79% won't admit it. 

The fix is boring and unglamorous: ask the right 8 to 12 questions, get the answers in one place, hand them to the gifter.

This page gives you three ways to do that. Fifty questions you can copy, grouped by what they actually tell your gifter. 

A printable PDF you can hand around the office. And an online template you can send via Slack, email, or WhatsApp in two clicks, so the responses land back in one shared spreadsheet instead of fifteen scattered DMs.


## Why Use a Secret Santa Questionnaire?

A Secret Santa questionnaire helps people understand what others like and don't like. This makes it easier to pick out meaningful gifts. It ensures everyone receives something they appreciate, creating a better experience for all involved.

## 20 Secret Santa questionnaire questions, grouped by what they tell your gifter

1. **What is your favorite color?**
   This helps gift-givers choose items in a color the recipient loves.

2. **Do you have any allergies?**
   It's crucial to avoid any allergens, especially if you're considering food or scented products.

3. **What are your hobbies?**
 Knowing someone's hobbies can lead to personalized gifts, like knitting supplies for a knitter. For the creatively inclined, using platforms such as **<a href="https://www.mixbook.com/">Mixbook</a>** offers a delightful way to capture and share memories with customized designs and themes.

4. **Do you prefer sweet or savory snacks?**
   This simple question helps narrow down snack options.

5. **What is your favorite type of music?**
   This can help if you're considering buying music-related gifts or memorabilia.

6. **Do you have a favorite movie or TV show?**
   Gifts related to their favorite shows or movies are always a hit.

7. **Are you a coffee or tea person?**
   This can guide you in choosing between coffee-related gifts or a variety of teas.

8. **What is your favorite book or author?**
   Perfect for choosing a book or literary-themed gift.

9. **Do you have any favorite sports teams?**
   Sports merchandise is a great gift for fans.

10. **What is your favorite holiday?**
    This can inspire themed gifts related to their favorite time of year.

11. **Do you have any pets?**
    Gifts for pets or pet-themed items can be a nice touch.

12. **What is your favorite type of cuisine?**
    This can help with choosing food-related gifts or even restaurant gift cards.

13. **Are there any particular scents you love or hate?**
    Important for choosing candles, lotions, or other scented products.

14. **Do you collect anything?**
    Knowing about any collections can lead to very special and appreciated gifts.

15. **What is your clothing size (if you feel comfortable sharing)?**
    Useful for clothing or accessory gifts, but make this optional.

16. **What is one thing you always carry with you?**
    This can inspire practical gifts that fit into their daily routine.

17. **Do you have any dietary restrictions?**
    Crucial for choosing food items, ensuring they are suitable.

18. **What is your favorite way to relax?**
    Gifts that help them relax, like bath products or a cozy blanket, are always appreciated.

19. **Do you prefer experiences or physical gifts?**
    Some people might prefer a ticket to an event or a class over a physical item.

20. **Is there something specific you’re hoping to receive?**
    This gives participants a direct hint and helps ensure everyone is happy with their gift.
    


    

## How to share your Secret Santa questionnaire in five minutes (online or printed)

### If you're going online (recommended for remote and hybrid teams)

1. Open the Formester Secret Santa template at [/templates/secret-santa-questionnaire-17483/](/templates/secret-santa-questionnaire-17483/). Click "Use template."

4. Hit "Share." Paste the link into Slack, Microsoft Teams, WhatsApp, or an email thread. Responses land in one dashboard you can sort, filter, and export to a spreadsheet.
5. When all responses are in, share each Santa their assigned person's answers privately. Done.

### If you're going printed (recommended for in-office, family, or school gift exchanges)

1. Download the printable PDF [insert link to PDF asset]. One file, multiple themed pages inside (standard, coworkers, adults, kids, family).
2. Print as many copies as you have participants.
3. Hand them out at the kickoff meeting, party, or class. Give people three days to return them.
4. Collect the filled forms in a folder, photograph or scan each one, and hand each Santa their assigned person's sheet.
5. Set a budget before the draw. The [Express Employment / Harris Poll December 2025 survey](https://www.expresspros.com/newsroom/news-releases/news-releases/2025/12/behind-the-holiday-glow-56-percent-of-employees-feel-gift-giving-pressure-and-nearly-half-say-it-hurts-their-wallet) put the typical US workplace Secret Santa [budget at $15 to $30 per person](https://flavor365.com/setting-the-perfect-secret-santa-budget-a-complete-guide/); pick one number and stick to it.

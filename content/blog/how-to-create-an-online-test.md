**Creating tests for students or employees** doesn’t have to be a time-consuming task. Paper-based tests take hours to prepare and grade, and they often fail to keep learners engaged. Online tests, on the other hand, are interactive, easy to manage, and provide instant insights into performance.

With **[Formester's AI test maker](/test-creator/)**, you can create any type of online test in minutes, share it anywhere, and even automate grading. This guide will show you step by step how to build a detailed online test that saves time, engages learners, and gives actionable results.

### Step 1: Log In and Choose Your Test Type

Start by logging into your Formester account, click Create New Form and Start with AI. You’ll have two main options:

- Scored Quiz – Automatically grades answers for quick assessment

- Outcome Quiz – Shows unique outcomes based on the answers

Choose the type that fits your purpose. Scored quizzes work well for objective assessments, while outcome quizzes are great for **[personality tests](/blog/how-to-create-mbti-16-personality-test/)**, skills assessments, or scenario-based learning.

### Step 2: Add Questions

Describe the test you want to create or paste your questions into the AI prompt. Click Generate, and within a minute, Formester’s AI will create a full test with all necessary questions and answers. This saves hours of manual preparation.

**You can further customize your test by:**

- Adding more questions using the **[AI Form Editor](/ai-form-generator/)**

- Using the Add Elements tab to include single choice, multiple choice, table choice, or picture-based questions

- Automatically numbering questions for easy organization

### Step 3: Make Your Test Interactive

**To boost engagement:**

- Embed images and videos for subjects like science, geography, or languages

- Use the one field per page layout so learners focus on one question at a time

- Add hints or feedback for better learning

Formester also supports automatic translation into 200 plus languages, making it ideal for diverse learners.

### Step 4: Automate Grading and Feedback

- **[Grading is fully automated](/blog/how-to-create-a-scored-quiz/)**. **You can:**

- Enable instant feedback

- Randomize answer options

- Track student or employee performance without manual work

All results appear in real time on your Formester dashboard. You can view detailed reports, highlight important submissions, and even add extra columns for additional data.

### Step 5: Share and Track Results

Once your test is ready, hit Publish and share the link via email, LMS, or social media. For advanced reporting, **[connect your test to Google Sheets](/integrations/google-sheets/)** to visualize trends and track performance over time.

**With Formester, online testing is fast**, engaging, and effortless. Whether you are a teacher or a trainer, this tool helps you save time while improving learning outcomes.

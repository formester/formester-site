## What is an AI Quiz Generator?
An AI quiz generator is a tool that makes quizzes automatically. It uses the topic or content you give it. 

You enter a simple prompt like **“Create a 10-question MCQ test on Thermodynamics.”** The **AI** will then create a test with suitable questions and answers.

It can create different types of questions, like multiple-choice and true/false. You can also use these tools to **[create outcome quizzes](/blog/how-to-create-an-outcome-quiz/)** and score quizzes within minutes.

## Why trivia quizzes work better than most icebreakers?

Because they get participation without forcing it. Nobody has to "share something vulnerable about themselves."

People just answer the question, and the funny ones surface naturally when half the room confidently says "Antarctica is the largest desert" and the other half says "the Sahara." (One of them is wrong; we'll get to it.)

The format also scales. A 10-person team and a 200-person all-hands can run the same quiz with different rules: small groups go round-robin, big groups go live with a [shareable Formester quiz link](/templates/funny-trivia-quiz-14818/) and a leaderboard. 

[Interactive quiz funnels convert 10 to 20 times higher than gated PDFs](https://getaiform.com/blog/agency-guide-replace-pdf-gating-quiz-funnels-interactive-lead-qualification-2026), which is also true at the icebreaker scale: people fill in a live link, they ignore a Word doc.

**[Trivia quizzes](https://www.creativityteaching.eu/trivia-quiz/)** aren’t just about showing off what you know (or don’t know!). They’re a great way to bring people together, make people laugh, and keep everyone entertained. 

* Trivia quizzes are perfect for connecting with others. Whether it's a family game night or a team-building activity at work, quizzes get everyone talking and having fun.

**Example:** At your family reunion, a quick trivia quiz helps break the ice and gets everyone laughing.

* **[Quizzes](/templates/categories/quizzes/)** turn learning into a game. You can pick up cool facts without the pressure of school tests.

**Example:** You may not have known that octopuses have three hearts, but a trivia quiz teaches you that while you’re having fun.

* Quizzes add a bit of friendly competition. It’s fun to show off your knowledge (or just guess and hope for the best).

**Example:** At a work party, your team goes head-to-head in a trivia contest, and the winner gets bragging rights.

* Trivia quizzes with funny questions help you unwind and laugh. They’re perfect after a long day.

**Example:** After a stressful day at work, you and your friends enjoy a trivia quiz, and suddenly everyone’s in a better mood.

### Round 1: Weird facts that aren't true facts (10)

1. **What's the only letter of the alphabet that doesn't appear in any US state name?** *Q.*
2. **What flavor of jelly bean was the result of a factory mix-up that turned into a Jelly Belly flagship?** *Buttered popcorn.*
3. **What was Coca-Cola's original color before food dye?** *Green.*
4. **What's the collective noun for a group of flamingos?** *A flamboyance.*
5. **What's the only food that never spoils?** *Honey.*
6. **What body part is the same size from age 5 to age 50?** *Your eyeballs.*
7. **What was the first item ever bought on the internet, in 1994?** *A Sting CD (Ten Summoner's Tales).*
8. **In which country is it illegal to keep a single pet goldfish alone in a bowl?** *Switzerland (goldfish are considered social animals).*
9. **What's the term for the dot above the lowercase letter "i" and "j"?** *A tittle.*
10. **Which is older: the Eiffel Tower or sliced bread?** *The Eiffel Tower (1889 vs sliced bread's first commercial sale in 1928).*

### Round 2: Animals doing animal things (10)

11. **Which animal has three hearts and blue blood?** *The octopus.*
12. **A group of owls is called what?** *A parliament.*
13. **Which bird can fly backward?** *The hummingbird.*

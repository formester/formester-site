Filling out forms can be boring and take a lot of time. This is especially true when users must enter the same information repeatedly.

Pre-filling some fields in **[feedback forms](/templates/categories/feedback-forms/)**, registration forms, or **[company surveys](/templates/categories/survey-forms/)** can save time. It can also reduce errors and make the experience better.

A pre-filled form automatically inserts specific information before it reaches the user. This makes the process faster and improves accuracy. In this guide, we’ll walk you through how to create a pre-filled form in Formester.

## What Are Pre-Fill Fields?
**[Pre-fill fields](/features/pre-fill-fields/)** allow you to pre-load information into specific fields of a form before sending it out. Instead of requiring users to manually enter these details, you fill them in beforehand to make the process smoother.

### Why Use Pre-Fill Fields?

- Save time by reducing the number of fields users need to complete.

- Make sure accuracy by pre-filling important details like names, emails, or project information.

- Improve user experience by making forms quicker and easier to fill.


### Examples of Pre-Filled Forms in Action
- **HR Performance Reviews:** HR teams can fill in employee names, job titles, and departments. This way, managers only need to assess performance.

- **Client Feedback for Agencies:** A marketing agency can fill in project details. This way, clients only need to give their feedback.

- **Event Registrations:** If you have attendee details, you can fill in their names and email addresses in the form.

### How to Create a Pre-Filled Form in Formester
Follow these easy steps below to pre fill your forms and survey with relevant information:

**Step 1: Select or Create Your Form**

- **[Log in to Formester](https://app.formester.com/users/sign_in)** and open the form you want to use.

- If you don’t have a form yet, you can:

- Create one from scratch.

- Use Formester’s **[AI Form Generator](/ai-form-generator/)** to generate a form with a simple prompt.

**Step 2: Access Pre-Fill Fields**
<iframe src="![a gif on navigating to pre-fill fields tab in formester](https://formester-strapi.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/a_gif_on_navigating_to_pre_fill_fields_tab_in_formester_e5c61b018e.gif)" height="300" width="100%"></iframe>

- Once your form or survey is published, head over to the **Share** tab.

- Then go to the **Pre-Fill Fields** tab.

**Step 3: Choose and Add Pre-Fill Details**
<iframe src="![a gif on pre-filling fields in formester](https://formester-strapi.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/a_gif_on_pre_filling_fields_in_formester_4d2d60306a.gif)" height="300" width="100%"></iframe>

- Click on Add pre fill form, 

- Select the fields you want to pre-fill,

- **Enter the values** you want to be automatically filled in the form.

**Example Scenario:** A marketing agency collecting client feedback can pre-fill:

- Project Name: "SEO Campaign 2025"

- Client Name: "John Doe"

- Email: "john@example.com"

- Project Duration: "6 months"

This way, when the client opens the **form**, they don’t need to enter this information themselves.

**Step 4: Generate the Pre-Filled Link**
<iframe src="![a gif on generating pre-filled form link in formester](https://formester-strapi.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/a_gif_on_generating_pre_filled_form_link_in_formester_8019f321cf.gif)" height="300" width="100%"></iframe>

- Once all necessary details are added, click on "Generate Form Link."

- Formester will create a **unique pre filled link** that includes the pre-filled details.

- You can copy and share this link **via email**.

**Step 5: Share and Review the Pre-Filled Form**

- Send the **pre-filled form link** to your audience.

- When they open it, the pre-filled fields will already have the correct details.

- The user only needs to complete the remaining fields before submitting the form.

Why Use Pre-Filled Forms?
Pre-filled forms are useful in many scenarios, from surveys to **business workflows**. Here’s how they help:

- **Saves Time:** Users don’t have to enter repetitive information.

- **Reduces Errors:** Pre-filling important details like names and emails prevents typos and mistakes.

- **Improves User Experience:** Forms become easier and faster to complete, increasing response rates.

- **Improves Data Consistency:** By pre-filling key details, you ensure accurate and standardized data collection.

### How to Use Pre-Filled Forms?
There are many ways businesses and individuals can use pre-filled forms. Here are a few real-world applications:

**Employee Performance Reviews**
HR teams carrying out performance reviews can Pre-fill employee name, department, and job title so managers only need to add ratings and comments.

**Client Feedback for Marketing Agencies**
A marketing agency that wants to collect **client feedback** on completed projects can Pre-fill project details (campaign name, duration, and objectives) so clients don’t have to enter them manually.

**Event Registrations & Surveys**
You’re **organizing an event** and need attendees to confirm details. You can Pre-fill attendee names, company names, and emails based on previous registrations.

**Order & Support Request Forms**
A business receives customer inquiries and needs users to **submit requests**. They can Pre-fill customer details (name, email, order number) to make the request process smooth.

### Best Practices for Pre-Filled Forms
To get the best results when using pre-filled forms, follow these tips:

- **Only pre-fill necessary fields:** Too much information can overwhelm users.

- **Test the pre-filled form before sharing:** Make sure that all the details appear correctly.

- **Use different links for different users:** Generate unique links for different customers or employees to personalize the experience.

- **Provide clear instructions:** Let users know what details are already filled and what they need to complete.

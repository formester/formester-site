Looking for the best **Qualtrics alternatives** in 2024? You’re in the right place! **[Qualtrics](https://www.qualtrics.com/)** is a powerful tool to build **[surveys](/templates/categories/survey-forms/)**, but it’s not for everyone, whether it's due to its high cost or customer support.

## Why Look for Qualtrics Alternatives?
Qualtrics is a powerful survey platform, but it’s not the right fit for everyone. Whether you're a small business, a growing startup, or just need something simpler, here’s why you might want to consider alternatives to Qualtrics:

**High cost for basic needs**
Qualtrics can be expensive, especially if you only need core survey features. Many alternatives offer similar tools at a much lower price or even for free.

**Too complex for simple surveys**
If all you want is to run a quick customer feedback or employee survey, Qualtrics may feel overwhelming with its steep learning curve and bulky setup.

**Packed with features you may never use**
From advanced analytics to enterprise-level logic, Qualtrics has a lot. But most users don’t need all of it, and those extras just add clutter.

**Long setup and training time**
Teams often need training or external help just to get started with Qualtrics. Alternatives offer faster onboarding and simpler interfaces.

**Not ideal for small teams or solo users**
If you’re a marketer, educator, or startup founder, Qualtrics might be overkill. Lighter tools give you what you need without the noise.

**Better UX available elsewhere**
Many Qualtrics alternatives are designed with user experience in mind, making it easier to create, share, and analyze surveys without technical skills.

**Limited flexibility in some pricing plans**
You may need to upgrade just to access features that other tools offer in their base plans.

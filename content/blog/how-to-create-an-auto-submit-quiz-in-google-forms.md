Do you want to create a quiz that **submits answers automatically** when time runs out? If you are using Google Forms, you might be wondering if this is even possible.

**[Google Forms](https://docs.google.com/forms/)** is great for making simple quizzes. You can add multiple choice questions, give points, and even show correct answers. 

But by default, Google Forms does not support automatic submission. That means users have to click the "**Submit**" button themselves.

Still, with a few smart workarounds, you can get close to an **auto-submit experience** using Google Forms and some extra tools. In this guide, we will walk you through how to do it.

### Why Use an Auto Submit Quiz?
Auto submit quizzes are useful when you want to:

- Set a time limit for quizzes

- Prevent users from changing answers after the timer ends

- Run timed tests for students

- Collect fast and fair responses

- Improve the quiz-taking experience

This is very helpful for teachers, trainers, and recruiters who want more control over time.

### What You Can Do in Google Forms
Google Forms itself does not offer a built-in timer or auto-submit feature. But you can still make it work by combining it with other tools like Google Classroom, add-ons, or custom scripts.

Here is how you can set it up.

### Step 1: Create the Quiz in Google Forms
Start by building your quiz.

- Create a new form

- Click the gear icon to open Settings

- Under the “Quizzes” tab, turn on “Make this a quiz”

- Add your questions and assign point values

- Decide whether to show results instantly or later

Make sure your form is ready before moving to the next step.

### Step 2: Set a Timer Using Add-ons
Since Google Forms does not have a timer, you can use a Google Forms add-on like "**Form Timer**" or "Quilgo."

**These tools allow you to:**

- Set a countdown timer

- Auto-close the form when time runs out

- Force users to submit within a certain time

**Here is how to use Form Timer:**

- Click the puzzle icon in your Google Form

- Search for and install “Form Timer”

- Follow the setup to connect your quiz with a countdown

- Share the special quiz link provided by Form Timer

Now, your quiz has a **timer** that controls how long someone can answer.

### Step 3: Let the Form Auto Submit
These add-ons will not press the “**Submit**” button for users, but they can:

- Automatically close the form

- Lock the screen after time ends

- Prevent late answers from being recorded

This is the closest you can get to auto-submitting a Google Form. It still requires users to click “Submit,” but the add-on limits what they can do once the timer ends.

**Limitations to Keep in Mind**

- Google Forms alone does not have auto submit

- You must rely on third-party add-ons

- Some tools require users to sign in with Google

- Timer control is limited and may not be fully secure

- You cannot track behavior like tab switching or cheating
